\begin{abstract}
The independently derived ALIGN contract constructs a detector-led common
sample identity, typed and bounded alignment operators, half-open scan
windows, explicit origin/validity state, full mapping response and covariance,
and original-sample exposure.  The assessed application source at
\AuditGoverningSHAPrint{} is nonconformant: it contains deterministic
invalid-success paths in HWPR timing, slot assignment, gap filling,
telescope-variable interpolation, observation lifecycle, scan slicing, and
persisted metadata, while the required mapping, uncertainty, provenance, and
validation evidence are absent.  The proposed contract therefore requires
amendment and owner approval; production remains existing-use-only, with the
affected new consumers and interpretations fail-closed.  This report does not
authorize repair, external execution, or production expansion.
\end{abstract}

\section{Independently claimed estimator}

The exact independently frozen estimator is the companion file identified in
the authority table; its SHA-256 is \AuditCoreSHAPrint.  It defines numbered
Equations A1--A19 and tests T01--T18.  In compact form:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2.2em]
\item \Aeq{1} maps every native clock to a declared detector reference clock
with a positive-add seconds offset applied once before alignment.
\item Equations A2--A3 define a detector-led common grid and an unambiguous
round-half-up slot map with strict tolerance, unique occupancy, and retained
native timestamp residual.
\item Equations A4--A5 distinguish bounded scalar interpolation from shortest-
arc circular interpolation; state/categorical variables use declared step
support or exact coincidence, never ordinary linear interpolation.
\item \Aeq{6} is the realized block selection/interpolation operator
$\boldsymbol y_q=A_q\boldsymbol x$; \Aeq{7} is bounded internal detector-gap
linear fill with no edge extrapolation and explicit source weights.
\item Equations A8--A9 define stable zero-based, half-open, nonoverlapping scan
windows that retain final partial and short identities.
\item Equations A10--A11 define conditional template/fractional-delay response;
Equations A12--A16 define full mapping, timing, model, and selection uncertainty.
\item \Aeq{17} gives zero acquired exposure to synthesized/unavailable values;
Equations A18--A19 define $Q$ and strict original-only alignment eligibility.
\end{enumerate}

ALIGN is one-pass, not iterative.  Its affine representation applies only
conditional on the realized mapping and away from circular wrap ambiguity;
circular interpolation is nonlinear with that local affine form.  It performs
no photometric, spatial, beam, WCS, or polarization estimator.

\section{Response, covariance, and exposure claim}

For fixed realized $A_q$, the proposed mean and full covariance are
\[
 \mathrm{E}[\boldsymbol y_q\mid A_q]=A_q\mathrm{E}[\boldsymbol x\mid A_q],
 \qquad C_{y_q\mid A_q}=A_qC_{x\mid A_q}A_q^{\mathsf T}.
\]
Linear interpolation preserves DC and affine ramps but has the fractional-
delay response in \Aeq{11}; gap fill is nonstationary and row-specific.
Timing/offset covariance, interpolation-model error, and policy/selection
uncertainty remain separate terms.  Missing covariance is unavailable, not
independent white noise.  Synthesized values may provide explicitly bounded
continuity support but contribute zero acquired detector exposure and no new
independent weight.  Scan elapsed span, nominal duration, acquired exposure,
valid-original exposure, and synthesized count are separate products.

The governing implementation retains neither the realized mapping weights nor
these covariance, response, and exposure components.  Finite synthesized values are
calibrated and filtered before flags are used for downstream selection, so a
binary flag does not remove their transfer into neighboring filtered samples.

\section{Exact implemented operators and known mismatches}

In the active real-data path, valid configuration requires timing-gap
interpolation.  Let $h=1/f_s$, $t_0$ be the maximum first time over KIDs
and optional raw HWPR streams, and $t_1$ the minimum last time.  The
implementation constructs
\[
 c_i=t_0+ih,\qquad N=\left\lfloor\frac{t_1-t_0}{h}\right\rfloor+1.
\]
It marks slot $i=\mathrm{round}[(t-t_0)/h]$ occupied when the residual is at
most $h/2$.  Scan-local detector rows are independently snapped to the
nearest common slot, with exact ties going left; multiple rows can overwrite
one slot.  Every unfilled internal slot is linearly filled between the nearest
filled common slots, and every leading/trailing slot is constant extrapolated.
There is no maximum run count or elapsed time.

Separately, the logged gap count is
\[
 G=\sum_{k=1}^{N_D-1}\mathbf 1\{Ts_{k,3}-Ts_{k-1,3}>1\},
\]
which counts transitions, not missing packets, common slots, elapsed gap
duration, or gap identity.  The exact half-slot inconsistency makes the mask
and numeric operator disagree even on their own chosen grid.  These are known
mismatches, not external-input assumptions that permit conditional conformity.
