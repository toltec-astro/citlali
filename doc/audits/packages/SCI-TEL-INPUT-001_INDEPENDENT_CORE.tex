\section{Typed structural objects}

All identities below are values, not filenames, row positions used as external
identity, or mutable process state.  A digest is computed over a declared byte
serialization and names its algorithm.  A path or modification time may be
recorded for diagnostics but is never sufficient identity.

\subsection{Raw telescope source identity}

For one immutable raw telescope source define
\[
R = (I_{obs}, I_{acq}, I_{file}, D_{file}, S_{nc}, V_{producer}),
\]
where:

\begin{itemize}
\item $I_{obs}$ is the external observation identity, including the complete
  producer identifiers needed to distinguish observations, subobservations,
  scans, interfaces, or coherent segments when present;
\item $I_{acq}$ is a typed acquisition-source identity.  It is structural
  lineage only and carries the typed state\newline \typedunavailable;
\item $I_{file}$ records the exact selected raw object, immutable size, and
  stable diagnostic locator;
\item $D_{file}$ is the digest of the complete raw file bytes;
\item $S_{nc}$ is a deterministic NetCDF structural fingerprint covering group
  paths, dimensions, variables, types, dimension vectors, attributes, storage
  properties, and raw typed payloads; and
\item $V_{producer}$ identifies the producer format/schema authority available
  at selection time.
\end{itemize}

Once resolved, $R$ is immutable.  Re-reading the same path and obtaining a
different size, digest, or structural fingerprint produces a new candidate
identity and invalidates every cache entry bound to the former $R$.

\subsection{Detector-input and reduction association}

Define the ordered detector-input identity
\[
K = (I_{obs}, [D_{kid,0},\ldots,D_{kid,m-1}], I_{order}, I_{assigned}),
\]
where each detector digest is immutable, $I_{order}$ binds the exact input
ordering, and $I_{assigned}$ binds the exact ALIGN/assigned-time implementation
authority, coherent segment, detector/sample ordering, realized cadence/rate
lattice, assigned values, requested/effective/realized offset or resampling
state, parent stage, and processing identity.  Its event state is unavailable.

Define a reduction association
\[
A = (I_{reduction}, I_{request}, R, K, I_{selection}, I_{association}).
\]
$I_{request}$ binds the accepted low-level Citlali request; $I_{selection}$
binds the deterministic candidate and tie/error policy; and
$I_{association}$ binds the exact observation and segment relationship between
the raw telescope source, ordered detector inputs, and this reduction.

The association \shall{} be resolved before recomputation or Citlali state
mutation.  Zero candidates, multiple candidates without an already-authorized
unique selection rule, mixed observation identities, ambiguous segment
membership, or a detector/telescope/reduction identity mismatch is a typed
failure.  Glob order, directory order, newest modification time, or ``first
file found'' is not an association rule.

\subsection{Recomputed product identity}

For one derived telescope product define
\[
P = (R,A,V_{app},V_{ast},V_{contract},C_{resolved},M,D_{product},Q),
\]
where $V_{app}$ is the exact producer application identity, $V_{ast}$ is the
accepted AST transform authority/version, $V_{contract}$ is this structural
contract and output schema version, $C_{resolved}$ is the complete
observation-resolved transform configuration, $M$ is the realized mutation
manifest, $D_{product}$ is the complete product digest, and $Q$ is the
completion and verification record.  Changing any tuple member creates a
different product identity.

$P$ is immutable after atomic completion.  A recomputed filename, file
existence, successful open, or plausible numeric content does not establish
$P$.

\section{Row model and invariants}

\subsection{Index, cardinality, and ordered record}

Let the authoritative telescope row dimension have cardinality $N$.  A valid
source has $N>0$ and zero-based ordered row indices
\[
i \in \{0,1,\ldots,N-1\}.
\]
The first row is exactly $i=0$ and the last row is exactly $i=N-1$; neither may
be skipped, synthesized, truncated, wrapped, or treated as a sentinel.

For each row define the structural record
\[
X_i=(i,T_i,G_i,H_i;\;Y_i),
\]
where $T_i$ is the recorded \code{TelTime} value and exact stored bits, $G_i$
is the complete ordered set of pointing-state inputs consumed by the accepted
AST authority, $H_i$ is \code{Hold}, and
\[
Y_i=(A^{par}_i,R^{src}_i,D^{src}_i),
\]
corresponding respectively to \code{ActParAng}, \code{SourceRaAct}, and
\code{SourceDecAct}.
This notation asserts row lineage only.  It does not state or rederive the
mapping $G_i,H_i,T_i\mapsto Y_i$.

The raw and recomputed product \shall{} have the same $N$, row order, dimension
identity, and first/last row.  Every $Y_i$ \shall{} be computed from and remain
paired with exactly $(T_i,G_i,H_i)$ at the same $i$.  No sorting, dropping,
insertion, padding, nearest-row replacement, forward/back fill, interpolation,
or one-row rotation is permitted in recomputation.

\subsection{Timestamp and cadence state}

Each timestamp carries its exact stored type, bits, declared unit, scale, and
clock/producer identity.  No unit is inferred from the variable name.  A
comparison in seconds is permitted only through an explicit recorded unit
conversion; it remains an assigned-grid comparison, not a physical-event
interpretation.

For adjacent finite assigned timestamps define
\[
\Delta_i=T_i-T_{i-1},\qquad 1\le i<N.
\]
The registered nominal telescope cadence is 20 ms.  ``Nominal'' is not a
tolerance.  The realized $\Delta_i$ sequence, finite status, exact duplicates,
non-monotonic values, and gaps \shall{} be recorded.  Acceptance requires an
exact, identity-bound cadence/gap policy.  This core does not invent its
tolerance; if no authorized policy exists, cadence-dependent admission
\failclosed{} rather than silently accepting approximate regularity.

The following states are distinct:
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{present\_finite\_ordered}: the row and timestamp are present,
  finite, and strictly later than the preceding row under the declared scale;
\item \code{gap}: the row-to-row interval violates the bound policy without
  asserting which physical event is absent;
\item \code{duplicate}: the exact assigned timestamp repeats;
\item \code{nonmonotonic}: the assigned timestamp decreases;
\item \code{missing}: required row identity is absent; and
\item \code{nonfinite}: a required timestamp or row value is non-finite under
  its declared validity rules.
\end{itemize}

Missing and unavailable are typed states.  They \mustnot{} be represented by an
undocumented NaN, zero, negative number, copied neighbor, or synthesized row.

\subsection{Hold transitions and coherent row pairing}

\code{Hold} is a row-valued state, not a file-level hint.  If
$H_{k-1}\ne H_k$, both sides of the transition and the outputs at $k-1$ and
$k$ \shall{} retain their original same-row pairing.  A transition never
authorizes shifting an output, choosing a different timestamp, or changing
AST mathematics.

Length and monotonicity alone cannot prove row pairing: an equal-length
plus-one or minus-one rotation may satisfy both.  Therefore the realized
product identity \shall{} include a deterministic row-lineage commitment over,
at minimum,
\[
(i,\mathrm{bits}(T_i),\mathrm{bits}(G_i),
 \mathrm{bits}(H_i),\mathrm{bits}(Y_i))
\]
for every $i$, bound to the trusted producer/AST identity and raw-parent
digest.  A self-reported digest recomputed after corruption is not independent
evidence of correct pairing.  Admission requires a trusted derivation record
or deterministic comparison capable of distinguishing the intended pairing
from coherent $\pm1$ row displacement.

\section{NetCDF preservation and mutation contract}

\subsection{Closed mutation allowlist}

The only variables that may be added or whose data may change are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \code{ActParAng};
\item \code{SourceRaAct}; and
\item \code{SourceDecAct}.
\end{enumerate}

This is a closed allowlist.  Each allowlisted variable \shall{} use the exact
authoritative telescope-row dimension and cardinality $N$, publish an explicit
type, unit, coordinate frame/epoch/convention identifier, missing/non-finite
policy, AST authority, and row-lineage identity, and have a value for every
row.  Current telescope angular convention expects radians unless the variable
explicitly states otherwise; a bare name never supplies a unit.  Coordinate
frame, epoch, RA wrapping, sign, handedness, and parallactic-angle conventions
come from accepted AST authority and are not chosen here.

If an allowlisted variable already exists, replacement is permitted only under
the exact output schema/version bound into $P$.  If it is absent, addition is
permitted only with that same schema.  An undocumented type, dimension,
attribute, fill, unit, or frame change is failure, not compatibility.

\subsection{Preservation operator}

For every dimension, group, and non-allowlisted variable, the transformation
is the identity operator.  ``Bitwise preservation'' means:

\begin{itemize}
\item identical group path, name, declared type, dimension vector and order;
\item identical dimension identity and cardinality, including unlimited-state
  metadata;
\item identical variable attributes and their typed payload bits;
\item identical storage semantics needed to recover the same typed array,
  including fill, endianness, chunking, compression, and checksums where
  represented; and
\item identical raw typed element bits in row-major logical order, including
  NaN payloads and signed zero.
\end{itemize}

Physical byte offsets or container layout may change when NetCDF is rewritten;
that does not weaken the element, metadata, ordering, or dimension invariants.
Conversely, numerically equal decoded values are insufficient when their
stored bits or required metadata differ.

Global attributes, group attributes, provenance containers, or helper
variables are not silently writable.  Required derivation provenance lives in
the immutable product/completion record $P,Q$.  Embedding new provenance in
NetCDF would expand the mutation class and requires an explicit successor to
this core; it is not inferred here.

The realized mutation manifest $M$ lists every added or changed allowlisted
variable, its before/after presence and digest, and proves the identity result
for everything outside the allowlist.  An unlisted mutation rejects the whole
candidate before admission.

\section{Cache, completion, retry, and provenance}

\subsection{Cache key and source/version binding}

A reusable cache key is a collision-resistant digest of a canonical
serialization of at least
\[
(R,A,V_{app},V_{ast},V_{contract},C_{resolved},
 \text{allowlist/schema identity}).
\]
The key includes exact raw bytes and structure, observation/detector/reduction
association, row ordering, assigned-grid identity, application and transform
versions, and complete resolved configuration.  Filename, directory, mtime,
file existence, and a partially matching tuple are never cache keys.

A candidate whose key or embedded completion record does not exactly match the
current request is \code{stale} and \shall{} be rejected.  Cache reuse records
the cache key, product digest, verification result, producer identity, raw
parent, completion identity, and reuse event in realized provenance.

\subsection{Atomic completion state machine}

The allowed states are:
\begin{center}
\code{absent} $\longrightarrow$ \code{writing\_private}
$\longrightarrow$ \code{verified\_private} $\longrightarrow$
\code{complete}.
\end{center}
Only \code{complete} is reusable or admissible.  Production occurs at a unique
private path inaccessible as a completed cache entry.  Before publication the
producer closes and flushes the NetCDF object, verifies $R,A,N$, row
invariants, mutation allowlist, preservation result, row-lineage commitment,
schema, and complete digest, and durably writes $Q$.  Publication makes the
verified data and matching completion record visible atomically under a
single-winner or content-identical concurrency rule.

A crash, cancellation, exception, writer collision, missing completion record,
digest mismatch, open private path, truncated NetCDF object, or validation
failure yields \code{partial} or \code{rejected}; it never advances to
\code{complete}.  A completion filename or marker cannot override missing
data, structural failure, or an error record.

\subsection{Retry and deterministic provenance}

A retry begins from the same immutable inputs or records a new identity.  It
does not append to, repair in place, or bless a private/partial candidate.
Private leftovers are quarantined or replaced through a separately named
temporary object.  Two successful retries from the same complete identity
tuple shall produce the same declared structural content, mutation manifest,
row-lineage commitments, and scientific values under the registered
determinism policy.  Volatile wall-clock diagnostics, if retained outside the
scientific identity, are explicitly classified and cannot affect admission.

The provenance chain is one-way:
\[
R\rightarrow A\rightarrow C_{resolved}\rightarrow P\rightarrow Q
\rightarrow\text{Citlali admission}.
\]
No later stage rewrites raw, requested, effective, or observation-resolved
identity to match what happened.

\section{Citlali admission contract}

Admission is a cold-boundary transaction.  All checks complete before any
live telescope, observation, scan, ALIGN, AST, RTC/PTC, map, diagnostic,
product, or provenance execution state is mutated.  Failure returns a typed
error through the session boundary and cannot be logged and forgotten.

\subsection{Required predicates}

Citlali may admit $P$ only if all of the following are true:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{C\arabic*.}]
\item \textbf{Identity.} $R,A,P,Q$ exactly match the accepted reduction,
  observation, ordered detector inputs, assigned-grid identity, application,
  AST authority, schema, and resolved request.
\item \textbf{Completion.} Atomic state is \code{complete}; all digests and
  completion records match; no stale, private, partial, or ambiguous candidate
  participates.
\item \textbf{Cardinality and order.} $N>0$; the telescope dimension, row
  indices, first and last rows, and order match the raw parent; no row is
  inserted, dropped, duplicated, reordered, or padded.
\item \textbf{Time structure.} Required timestamps are present and finite,
  strictly ordered, and evaluated under the exact accepted cadence/gap policy;
  all duplicate, non-monotonic, missing, non-finite, and gap states are
  explicitly dispositioned.  No physical-event meaning is inferred.
\item \textbf{Coverage.} The telescope assigned-time domain covers every
  detector assigned-time value and support point required by the exact
  registered ALIGN interface for the coherent segment.  The ALIGN-owned
  interpolation, slicing, gap, edge, and support response is not rederived.
  Missing policy identity or insufficient support fails closed.
\item \textbf{Coherent pairing.} For every $i$, \code{TelTime}, pointing-state
  inputs, \code{Hold}, \code{ActParAng}, \code{SourceRaAct}, and
  \code{SourceDecAct} share the same row lineage.  The verification mechanism
  can reject a coherent equal-length $\pm1$ output displacement.
\item \textbf{Mutation and preservation.} $M$ contains only the three
  allowlisted variables, their schemas are exact, and every other variable and
  dimension satisfies the preservation operator.
\item \textbf{Units, frames, and validity.} Every required variable carries an
  accepted type, unit, frame/epoch/convention, shape, indexing, and
  missing/non-finite policy.  No bare value receives an inferred unit or
  validity.
\item \textbf{Unavailable event state.} $P$, $Q$, and admitted observation
  state explicitly carry \nolinkurl{physical_event_semantics} set to
  \code{unavailable} and bind the exact assigned-grid identity.  No detector
  time is promoted to an absolute oracle.
\end{enumerate}

Admission is all-or-nothing.  A failure leaves the prior run/observation state
unchanged, prevents scientific execution for this candidate, identifies the
failed predicate and object identity, and propagates as input/execution
failure.  A finite or plausible downstream value cannot promote a failed row
or product.

\subsection{One-way state resolution}

The telescope-ingress state is partitioned as follows:

observation-resolved state and cannot inherit a previous observation's source,
cache, transform, cadence, validity, or admission decision.

\subsection{Row and product validity}

Validity is separate from finiteness.  Each row records, without overloading a
numeric sentinel:

\begin{itemize}
\item raw-parent presence and identity validity;
\item timestamp presence, finiteness, order, cadence/gap classification;
\item pointing-state and \code{Hold} presence/validity;
\item recomputation availability and accepted AST identity;
\item each output's presence, finiteness, unit/frame validity, and same-row
  lineage; and
\item the product-level completion, preservation, cache, association, and
  admission result.
\end{itemize}

\code{verified\_original}, \code{verified\_recomputed}, \code{missing},
\code{nonfinite}, \code{unavailable}, and \code{rejected} are not
interchangeable.  Downstream consumers preserve raw-parent and row validity
separately from their local numerical support and output validity.

absolute detector/telescope physical phase, a sample centroid, a half- or
whole-sample correction, detector time as an absolute oracle, sub-sample
astrometric placement, timing-sensitive source-mask fidelity, or a precision,
accuracy, or response conclusion materially dependent on those meanings.

If a consumer requires any unavailable meaning, the request fails with a typed
dependency state.  It may not fall back silently to the assigned grid, a
neighboring row, a guessed offset, or a finite output.

\section{Core completion criteria}

This independent structural core is internally complete when:

\begin{enumerate}
\item raw source, detector/reduction association, recomputed product, and
  assigned-grid identities are explicit and immutable;
\item row cardinality, ordering, first/last row, timestamps, cadence/gaps,
  duplicates, missing/non-finite state, Hold transitions, and coherent pairing
  have fail-closed rules;
\item the three-variable mutation allowlist and identity preservation operator
  are closed;
\item cache binding, atomic completion, retry, stale/partial rejection, and
  deterministic provenance are explicit;
\item Citlali admission is transactional and produces typed failure;
\item requested, effective, observation-resolved, and realized state are
  one-way and separately recorded;
\item all preregistered displacement cases have a derived structural
  consequence; and
\item \typedunavailable{} and every corresponding downstream restriction are
  preserved without selecting a physical interpretation.
\end{enumerate}

Freezing this document does not assert that either implementation satisfies
it.  The first implementation-source or post-core evidence opening may occur
only after coordinator acknowledgement of the dedicated core commit.  Any
correction to frozen bytes is a preserved successor, not an edit of this core.
