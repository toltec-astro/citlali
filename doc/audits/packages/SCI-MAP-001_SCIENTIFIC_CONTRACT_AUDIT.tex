\begin{abstract}
The independently derived contract identifies ordinary naive mapmaking as a
selected, normalized linear gridding estimator and observation coaddition as a
second, conditionally inverse-variance, image-domain weighted mean.  At exact
source SHA \AuditGoverningSHA, the finite sequential naive signal and
kernel path conforms to that estimator.  The package as implemented is
nevertheless \emph{nonconformant}: a supported detector-parallel path contains
shared-scalar data races; the persisted support mask admits zero and non-finite
weights in allowed states; coaddition does not fail closed on WCS or map-slot
identity mismatch; and naive coadd noise can bypass the observation support
operator applied to signal.  In the same allowed naive-coadd configuration,
requesting empirical noise products makes raw observation publication fail
before coadd accumulation.  Formal-weight, response, exposure, lifecycle, and
covariance claims are also under-specified, and required MAP-specific local and
Unity evidence remains absent.  Package identity remains coherent, so the verdict is
\texttt{amend}; existing accepted operation is preserved as
\code{existing_use_only}, while new significance, covariance, response, or
feedback interpretations fail closed.
Post-freeze cross-audit evidence reported bitwise-identical raw products in a
strict convolve control/candidate comparison while a later filtered product
admitted extreme guard-band standardized amplitudes outside a reconstructed
raw science-valid region.  That evidence establishes no raw mapmaking
regression and closes no finding.  It does make the missing authoritative,
reproducible raw-validity handoff in F002/F007/F008/F010 a demonstrated
consumer risk; the filtered estimator itself remains outside this audit.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Selected linear system and generalized least squares}

After eligibility selection, unit conversion, and projection are fixed, write
\begin{align}
  \boldsymbol d &= X\boldsymbol s+\boldsymbol b+\boldsymbol n,
  &\Cov(\boldsymbol n\mid\Theta)&=C_n,
  \label{eq:audit-selected-system}\\
  \widehat{\boldsymbol s}_{\rm GLS}
    &= (X^{\mathsf T}C_n^{-1}X)^{+}
       X^{\mathsf T}C_n^{-1}(\boldsymbol d-\boldsymbol b),
  \label{eq:audit-gls}\\
  C_{\widehat s\mid\Theta}
    &= (X^{\mathsf T}C_n^{-1}X)^{+}
       X^{\mathsf T}C_n^{-1}C_n C_n^{-1}X
       (X^{\mathsf T}C_n^{-1}X)^{+}.
  \label{eq:audit-gls-cov}
\end{align}
The pseudoinverse is on the identifiable subspace.  Null modes, additive bias,
calibration covariance, and pointing/response covariance remain explicit; a
diagonal weight plane is not Equation~\eqref{eq:audit-gls-cov}.

\subsection{Ordinary naive normalized gridding}

Let \(I_p\) contain only eligible \((t,d)\) with \(M_{td}=1\), valid projection
into \(p\), finite required signal/response/exposure coefficients, and an
allowed finite weight.  Define \(q_{ptd}=P_{ptd}w_{td}\) only on \(I_p\);
\(M\) is a selector, never an arithmetic zero mask.  The signal numerator,
denominator, and stored map are
\begin{align}
 n_p &= \sum_{(t,d)\in I_p}q_{ptd}d_{td},
 & D_p &= \sum_{(t,d)\in I_p}q_{ptd},
 \label{eq:audit-naive-num}\\
 \widehat m_p &= \begin{cases}n_p/D_p,&D_p\text{ finite and }D_p>0,\\
                              \text{invalid},&\text{otherwise},
                 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:audit-naive-map}\\
 n^{(k)}_p &= \sum_{(t,d)\in I_p}q_{ptd}k_{td},
 & \widehat k_p &= n^{(k)}_p/D_p
      \quad(D_p\text{ finite and }D_p>0).
 \label{eq:audit-kernel}
\end{align}
For fixed selection, the map operator is
\(A_{p,td}=q_{ptd}/D_p\) on support.  Therefore
\begin{align}
  \E[\widehat{\boldsymbol m}\mid\Theta]
    &=A\E[\boldsymbol d\mid\Theta]
      =R\boldsymbol s+\boldsymbol b_m,
  &R&=AX,\qquad \boldsymbol b_m=A\boldsymbol b,
  \label{eq:audit-response}\\
  C_{m\mid\Theta}&=A C_n A^{\mathsf T}.
  \label{eq:audit-naive-cov}
\end{align}
If \(P\) is one-hot, selected samples are independent, and
\(w_{td}=\sigma_{td}^{-2}\), then
\begin{equation}
  \Var(\widehat m_p\mid\Theta)=D_p^{-1},\qquad
  q^{\rm formal}_p=D_p.
  \label{eq:audit-formal-weight}
\end{equation}
Without every condition in Equation~\eqref{eq:audit-formal-weight}, \(D_p\) is
a normalization/gridding weight, not authorized inverse variance.  Shared
detector noise, preprocessing, non-one-hot projection, selection, and fitted
weights generally make Equation~\eqref{eq:audit-naive-cov} non-diagonal.

\subsection{Response, hits, exposure, support, and validity}

For a template \(\boldsymbol\tau\) with amplitude \(\alpha\),
\begin{equation}
 R_{\tau}=\frac{\partial\E[\widehat{\boldsymbol m}\mid\Theta]}
                 {\partial\alpha}=AX\boldsymbol\tau.
 \label{eq:audit-template-response}
\end{equation}
A mapped kernel equals this response only if its input tracer is exactly
\(X\boldsymbol\tau\) through the same fixed selection and preprocessing.
Constant, delta, beam/template, resolved, extended, variable-coverage, and edge
responses are distinct tests; normalized gridding preserves a constant only
where the selected operator has unit row sum and no upstream null removes it.

Let \(E_p\) be the upstream-eligible, finite projection/exposure set before any
signal-specific support decision.  Keep the following objects separate:
\begin{align}
 h_p &= \sum_{(t,d)\in E_p}{\bf1}[P_{ptd}\ne0],
 &h^{(w)}_p&=\sum_{(t,d)\in I_p}{\bf1}[q_{ptd}>0],
 \label{eq:audit-hits}\\
 x_p &= \sum_{(t,d)\in E_p}G^{(x)}_{ptd}\Delta t_{td},
 &S_p&={\bf1}[D_p\text{ finite and }D_p>0]\,S^{\rm policy}_p,
 \label{eq:audit-coverage}\\
 V_p &= S_p\,{\bf1}[\widehat m_p\text{ finite}]
       {\bf1}[\text{all required companions finite}]\nonumber\\
     &\quad{}\times{\bf1}[\text{identity/WCS/unit/response valid}].
 \label{eq:audit-validity}
\end{align}
Exposure may be positive where policy support is false.  Support may be true
without a formal variance claim.  Validity additionally requires finite values
and compatible identity.

\subsection{Observation coaddition}

Let \(\chi_{op}\) be the admissible contribution indicator after applying
registration \(J_o\), optional centering \(L_o\), and validity, and let
\(O_p=\{o:\chi_{op}=1\}\).  Invalid values are excluded from \(O_p\), never
multiplied by zero.  The current ordinary target is
\begin{align}
 N^{c}_p &= \sum_{o\in O_p}u_{op}(J_oL_o\widehat m_o)_p,
 &Q^{c}_p &= \sum_{o\in O_p}u_{op},
 \label{eq:audit-coadd-num}\\
 \widehat m^{c}_p&=\begin{cases}
   N^{c}_p/Q^{c}_p,&Q^{c}_p\text{ finite and }Q^{c}_p>0,\\
   \text{invalid},&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
 \label{eq:audit-coadd-map}
\end{align}
With stacked observation vector \(\boldsymbol z_m\), realized linear operator
\(B\), and full observation-map covariance \(C_m\),
\begin{align}
 C_{c\mid\Theta}&=B C_m B^{\mathsf T},
 &R_c&=B R_m.
 \label{eq:audit-coadd-cov}
\end{align}
Only when registered observation errors are mutually independent and
\(u_{op}=\Var(\widehat m_{op}\mid\Theta)^{-1}\) does
\begin{equation}
 \Var(\widehat m^c_p\mid\Theta)^{-1}=\sum_o u_{op}=Q^c_p.
 \label{eq:audit-coadd-precision}
\end{equation}
For correlated observations with pixel covariance \(C_p\), the scalar GLS
coadd is instead
\begin{equation}
 \widehat m^c_p=\frac{{\boldsymbol1}^{\mathsf T}C_p^{-1}\boldsymbol m_p}
                       {{\boldsymbol1}^{\mathsf T}C_p^{-1}\boldsymbol1},
 \qquad
 \Var(\widehat m^c_p)=
   ({\boldsymbol1}^{\mathsf T}C_p^{-1}\boldsymbol1)^{-1}.
 \label{eq:audit-correlated-coadd}
\end{equation}
The companion kernel/response shares \(\chi,J\) and a declared \(L\) wherever
that centering operator is part of the signal response, on \(Q^c_p>0\):
\(\widehat k^c_p=\sum_{o\in O_p}u(JL\widehat k_o)_p/Q^c_p\).  Exposure shares the
contribution and registration decisions \(\chi,J\), but never a signal-centering
operator: \(x^c_p=\sum_{o\in O_p}(Jx_o)_p\).  A contributing-observation count is a
hits product, not \(Q^c\) or \(x^c\).

\subsection{Noise-realization boundary}

For realization \(r\), the required same-operator test is
\begin{equation}
 \widehat{\boldsymbol m}^{(r)}=A\boldsymbol d^{(r)},\qquad
 \widehat{\boldsymbol m}^{c,(r)}=B\widehat{\boldsymbol m}^{(r)},
 \label{eq:audit-noise-operator}
\end{equation}
with the same eligibility, projection, weights, denominators, registration,
support, centering, and observation contribution set as signal, unless a
different operator is explicitly named and interpreted.  This is only an
operator-identity test; the randomness, independence, exchangeability, and
empirical calibration belong to \code{SCI-NOI-001/002}.

\subsection{Finite-state and boundary behavior}

Finite out-of-grid samples are dropped without wrapping or clamping.  Zero,
negative, and non-finite detector weights and non-finite signal samples are
skipped by ordinary accumulation.  Low positive weights are accepted, then a
data-derived policy floor may remove their pixels during normalization.
Coordinates are not screened before rounding, kernel values are not screened
before accumulation, and the primitive itself does not validate sample rate;
the governed high-level path does validate positive finite sample rate.  An
invalid value multiplied by a zero mask remains invalid, so neither observation
normalization nor coadd multiplication is a universal non-finite guard.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em]
\item \code{signal_I}: normalized observation estimator \(n/D\) or coadd

\item \code{weight_formal_I}: snapshot of the post-normalization-support map

Equations~\eqref{eq:audit-formal-weight} or

\item \code{weight_I}: the same weight when empirical application is absent,

support, or gain.

extension names.

These are two axes, not one ordered four-state enum.  On the raw scientific
axis, a pixel is (a) science-valid when \(V_p=1\), (b) retained low-confidence

normalization, finiteness, companion, or bundle admission fails.  Numerical
computability/population is a separate operator-local Boolean: raw mapmaking or
a later stencil may produce a finite number at a science-invalid coordinate.

raw-science-invalid combination.

At the governing SHA, the \(c/10\) normalization floor and full-\(c\) Boolean
threshold create two algorithmic regions, but the product contract neither
names the retained-low-confidence state nor persists both decisions exactly.
Thus the distinction is proposed and diagnostically inferable, not already
explicit/correct (F007/F010, with F002 for the Boolean).  NOI/FLT must keep
science validity and operator computability as separate masks and may never
let finite output promote invalid input.

\code{signal_I} is an amplitude estimator only on an approved \(V_p\) and

\code{weight_formal_I} may be treated as diagonal inverse variance only under

\code{coverage_bool_I} can at most be audited against its stage-local threshold

\emph{Disposition:} conditional formal-weight arithmetic is already explicit
in the proposed contract, but current inverse-variance metadata is F006 and
support/validity semantics are F002/F007/F010.  NOI must prohibit significance
or calibration outside an approved raw domain and must establish covariance;
FLT must intersect its separately defined numerical support with authoritative
raw validity rather than replace the latter.

downstream operator cannot construct the raw science-valid input mask exactly
