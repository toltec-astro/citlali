\subsection{Alignment boundary}

\begin{equation}
  \mathbf x^A=A_{\alpha}\mathbf x^{\rm acq}+\mathbf a_{\alpha},
  \tag{RTC-03}\label{eq:align}
\end{equation}
where $A_\alpha$ may include admitted interpolation or continuity-surrogate
weights and $\mathbf a_\alpha$ covers any declared affine term.  Normal
one-to-one original-valid mapping is implicit; exception intervals identify

ALIGN-synthesized cells may supply only their approved continuity role and

\subsection{Calibration before replacement}

For a detector $d$ and slot $j$, define a raw-to-calibrated multiplier
\begin{equation}
 g_{dj}=g^{\rm flx}_{d}\,
        g^{\rm ext}_{dj}\,g^{\rm unit}_{dj},\qquad
 g^{\rm ext}_{dj}=\exp\!\left[\tau_{b(d),j}X_j\right],
 \tag{RTC-04}\label{eq:gain}
\end{equation}
only on the approved positive finite model domain.  The exact continuous
atmosphere representation, band, reference plane, source artifact, and model
version are part of $g$.  Initially $g^{\rm unit}=1$ for top-of-atmosphere
mJy/beam.  The \code{sens} field is not in equation~\eqref{eq:gain}; \code{fcf}
cannot substitute for the factorization.

The calibration operator is
\begin{equation}
  x^C_{dj}=g_{dj}x^A_{dj}+q^C_{dj},
  \qquad \mathbf x^C=C_g\mathbf x^A+\mathbf q^C,
  \tag{RTC-05}\label{eq:cal}
\end{equation}
where any additive term $q^C$ must be explicitly named and is zero only when
the contract proves no additive calibration correction is applied.  Missing,
duplicate, conflicting, nonfinite, or unproven detector/APT associations make
$g_{dj}$ unavailable and fail the calibrated output.

The candidate scientific order is ALIGN $\rightarrow$ calibration
$\rightarrow$ replacement.  Let relative responsivity be defined for this
core by the forward convention
\begin{equation}
 x^{\rm raw}_{dj}=\rho_d s_{dj}+b_{dj},
 \quad [\rho_d]=U^{\rm raw}_d/[s].
 \tag{RTC-06}\label{eq:respdef}
\end{equation}
If the persisted field uses the reciprocal convention, Phase 2 must apply and
record that adapter rather than reuse the same symbol.  For donor $q$ replacing
target $d$ in the calibrated domain, the multiplicative conversion required
to equal raw-domain target substitution is
\begin{equation}
 \gamma_{d\leftarrow q,j}
   =\frac{g_{dj}\rho_d}{g_{qj}\rho_q},\qquad
 g_{dj}\left(\frac{\rho_d}{\rho_q}x^A_{qj}\right)
   =\gamma_{d\leftarrow q,j}x^C_{qj}
   \quad(q^C=0).
 \tag{RTC-07}\label{eq:donorscale}
\end{equation}
With additive terms, equation~\eqref{eq:donorscale} requires the corresponding
offset conversion.  Therefore an implementation that replaces before
calibration can be scientifically equivalent to this order only if it proves
equation~\eqref{eq:donorscale}, including factor direction, additive terms,
time dependence, units, and validity.  Simple operator commutation must not be
assumed.

\subsection{Despike detection, donor selection, and replacement}

Let $p_{dj}\in\{0,1\}$ be the realized target-replacement indicator.  Let
$\mathcal Q_{dj}$ be the realized donor cells and $w_{dj,q\ell}$ their weights.
Conditioned on selection state
$\omega=(p,\mathcal Q,w,\gamma,\beta)$, define
\begin{equation}
 x^R_{dj}=(1-p_{dj})x^C_{dj}
  +p_{dj}\left[
    \sum_{(q,\ell)\in\mathcal Q_{dj}}
      w_{dj,q\ell}\gamma_{d\leftarrow q,j\ell}x^C_{q\ell}
    +\beta_{dj}\right],
 \quad \sum_{q,\ell}w_{dj,q\ell}=1
 \ \hbox{when normalization is claimed}.
 \tag{RTC-08}\label{eq:replace}
\end{equation}
The offset $\beta$ covers a declared baseline or additive-factor conversion;
it is not presumed zero.  In vector form,
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf x^R=B_{\omega}\mathbf x^C+\mathbf b_{\omega}.
 \tag{RTC-09}\label{eq:replacevec}
\end{equation}
The detector that generates $p$, candidate eligibility, neighbor topology,
donor score, tie rule, use of source masks/flags, same-time versus neighboring
time selection, and fallback are scientific policy identities.  Once fixed,
equation~\eqref{eq:replacevec} is affine.  Because $\omega$ is normally derived
from the observed data, the unconditional operation is data-dependent and is
not generally linear, unbiased, stationary, or Gaussian.

A replaced target retains its target sample identity but links every donor and
the exact target/donor response factors.  It is numerically computable only if
all required donors and factors are admitted and finite.  It is not the
original target acquisition and is correlated with every reused donor and
every target sharing that donor.  Until a consumer-specific response and
covariance contract admits it, replacement is continuity-only, has zero direct
independent science weight/exposure, and remains visibly flagged.  A fallback
that leaves the spike unchanged, inserts zero, copies an invalid donor, or
silently clears a flag is not equivalent.

\subsection{FIR, IIR, and notch stages}

For each selected stage $k=1,\ldots,K$, record the stage type, order, exact
coefficients, coefficient design provenance, normalization, precision,
direction, boundary extension, initial/final state, mask operator, and finite
policy.  A time-invariant FIR stage has
\begin{equation}
 x^{F_k}_{d,j}=\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal L_k}
     h^{(k)}_{d,\ell}\,x^{F_{k-1}}_{d,j-\ell},
 \qquad H^{(k)}_d(e^{i\omega})=
     \sum_{\ell\in\mathcal L_k}h^{(k)}_{d,\ell}e^{-i\omega\ell}.
 \tag{RTC-10}\label{eq:fir}
\end{equation}
Its DC normalization is $H^{(k)}_d(1)=\sum_\ell h^{(k)}_{d,\ell}$, not a
label such as ``normalized.''  A causal IIR or notch stage is represented
without hidden history by
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf s_{j+1}=A_k\mathbf s_j+B_k u_j,
 \qquad v_j=C_k\mathbf s_j+D_k u_j,
 \qquad \mathbf s_{j_0}=\mathbf s_{0,k},
 \tag{RTC-11}\label{eq:iir}
\end{equation}
or by an exactly equivalent, coefficient-convention-qualified recurrence.
Stability requires poles strictly inside the unit circle for a causal stable
claim.  Forward/backward filtering, state carry across scans/chunks, state
reset, steady-state initialization, reflected padding, and terminal-state
handling are distinct operators.

Mask handling is part of the operator.  For example, normalized masked FIR
convolution would be
\begin{equation}
 v_j=\frac{\sum_\ell h_\ell m_{j-\ell}u_{j-\ell}}
            {\sum_\ell h_\ell m_{j-\ell}},
 \quad \left|\sum_\ell h_\ell m_{j-\ell}\right|>\epsilon_m,
 \tag{RTC-12}\label{eq:maskedfir}
\end{equation}
with an explicit denominator rule.  Filtering a filled stream and restoring a
mask, breaking into valid segments, zero-filling, renormalizing as above, and
holding filter state through a mask are not interchangeable.  Equation
\eqref{eq:maskedfir} is time-varying and data-dependent when $m$ is derived
from data or coordinates, even if $h$ is fixed.

Nonfinite values are never silently converted to zero.  Each stage must either
reject required nonfinite input, mark the affected output unavailable with its
exact transitive footprint, or apply one explicitly authorized replacement
operator before filtering.  A NaN that poisons an IIR state must invalidate
the state-dependent tail unless a declared reset/recovery rule proves a finite
restart.

Let $\mathbf c_{dj}$ be the continuous coordinate associated with detector
$d$ and slot $j$, constructed from admitted aligned pointing and focal-plane
association under AST.  Let source $a$ have coordinate $\mathbf c_a$ in the
same declared frame/epoch.  A generic realized source mask is
\begin{equation}
 m^{\rm src}_{dj}=
 \mathbb I\!\left[
 V^{\rm coord}_{dj}=1\ \land\
 \exists a:\;d_{\mathcal F}(\mathbf c_{dj},\mathbf c_a)
     \le r_a
 \right],
 \tag{RTC-13}\label{eq:sourcemask}
\end{equation}
where $d_{\mathcal F}$, topology, radius/shape, inclusive boundary, source
catalog, frame $\mathcal F$, detector binding, and any temporal dilation are
all recorded.  Point/OOF/Beammap ordinarily use admitted native AltAz
tangent coordinates; Science ordinarily uses admitted equatorial J2000 TAN.
No implicit frame conversion is authorized.

Coordinate-invalid is not equivalent to outside-source and may not be encoded
as $m^{\rm src}=0$.  If a required coordinate is invalid, unavailable,
out-of-domain, or ambiguously associated, the coordinate-dependent mask and
affected reduction fail closed.  Valid-but-outside-product support remains a
separate state.  A source mask is a processing exclusion/control, not an
acquisition fact, detector-validity flag, variance, or confidence value.

\subsection{Downsampling and decimation}

Let the final pre-decimation stream be $u_{d,j}$.  For integer factor $M\ge1$
and phase $p\in\{0,\ldots,M-1\}$,
\begin{equation}
 y_{d,n}=u_{d,p+nM},\qquad
 0\le p+nM<N,\qquad f_{\rm out}=f_{\rm in}/M.
 \tag{RTC-14}\label{eq:decimate}
\end{equation}
If an output is a block aggregate rather than point selection, its exact
weights replace equation~\eqref{eq:decimate}; the code name
``downsample'' cannot decide this.  The nominal output coordinate is
\begin{equation}
 t^{\rm out}_n=t_0+(p+nM)\Delta t,
 \tag{RTC-15}\label{eq:outtime}
\end{equation}
only when that point is the declared timestamp convention.  A weighted-center,
start, end, or integration-midpoint convention must be recorded instead and
must retain the full contributing support.  Odd/even $M$, first and last
partial blocks, and phase changes can alter cardinality and centering.

Decimation requires a declared anti-alias response before selection.  If the
preceding chain does not sufficiently suppress content above
$f_{\rm out}/2$, an alias-free signal claim is unavailable.  Downsampling does
not itself provide anti-aliasing.  Flags and validity over the contributing
impulse/block footprint require exact aggregation rules; ``any,'' ``all,''
bitwise OR, fractional support, and center-only are different policies.

\subsection{Claimed complete operator}

The preregistered interface operator is
\begin{equation}
 \boxed{
 \mathbf y=
 D_{M,p}\,F_{K,\theta_K}\cdots F_{1,\theta_1}\,
 B_{\omega}\left[C_g(A_{\alpha}\mathbf x^{\rm acq}
 +\mathbf a_{\alpha})+\mathbf q^C\right]
 +\hbox{declared affine state terms}}
 \tag{RTC-16}\label{eq:claimed}
\end{equation}
with chronological order read from right to left.  Mask, flag, context, and
state choices are components of $\theta_k$ and $\omega$, not annotations
outside the mathematics.  Conditional on all selections and states, the
operator has affine form
\begin{equation}
 \mathbf y=L_{\Omega}\mathbf x^{\rm acq}+\mathbf b_{\Omega},
 \quad
 L_{\Omega}=D_{M,p}F_{K}\cdots F_1B_{\omega}C_gA_{\alpha},
 \tag{RTC-17}\label{eq:affine}
\end{equation}
where $\Omega=(\alpha,g,\omega,\theta_{1:K},M,p,\hbox{windows})$ is the full
realized state.  Equation~\eqref{eq:affine} is not an unconditional LTI claim.

\section{Expectation, variance, covariance, and uncertainty}

\subsection{Conditional statistical moments}

For a declared input mean $\boldsymbol\mu_x$ and full statistical covariance
$\Sigma_x$, conditional on a fixed realized state independent of the random
noise,
\begin{align}
 \E[\mathbf y\given\Omega]
   &=L_{\Omega}\boldsymbol\mu_x+\mathbf b_{\Omega},
 \tag{RTC-18}\label{eq:mean}\\
 \Sigma^{\rm stat}_{y\mid\Omega}
   &=L_{\Omega}\Sigma_xL_{\Omega}^{\mathrm T}.
 \tag{RTC-19}\label{eq:cov}
\end{align}
The formal marginal variance is
$v^{\rm stat}_{a}=(\Sigma^{\rm stat}_{y\mid\Omega})_{aa}$; a conditional
inverse variance is $1/v_a$ only when $0<v_a<\infty$.  It is not the diagonal
of the full precision matrix, does not erase off-diagonal covariance, and does
not supply total uncertainty.  Reused donors, overlapping FIR support, IIR
memory, alignment interpolation, and decimation footprints naturally produce
off-diagonal and cross-detector terms.

If $\Sigma_x$ is unavailable, the full output covariance is unavailable.
White, diagonal, stationary, or independent detector noise must not be
invented.  A supplied validated covariance can be propagated for a requested
bounded subset through the factorized operator; no dense observation-sized
matrix is required as a standard product.

\subsection{Data-dependent selection}

When spike detection, donor selection, masking, coefficient fitting, state
initialization, or flagging depends on the same random data, conditioning on
$\Omega$ can create selection bias.  The unconditional moments obey
\begin{align}
 \E[\mathbf y]&=\E_{\Omega}\!\left[
   \E(\mathbf y\mid\Omega)\right],
 \tag{RTC-20}\label{eq:totalmean}\\
 \Cov(\mathbf y)&=\E_{\Omega}\!\left[
   \Cov(\mathbf y\mid\Omega)\right]
  +\Cov_{\Omega}\!\left[
   \E(\mathbf y\mid\Omega)\right].
 \tag{RTC-21}\label{eq:totalcov}
\end{align}
Equations~\eqref{eq:mean}--\eqref{eq:cov} alone are therefore only conditional
formal statistics.  An exact unconditional calculation requires the joint law
of the samples and selectors.  If that law is not supplied, selection bias and
selection covariance are explicitly unavailable rather than assumed zero.

\subsection{Calibration, factor, and response systematics}

Let $\boldsymbol\eta$ contain named nuisance parameters: detector
\code{flxscale}, common calibrator scale, pointing-derived scale correction,
opacity/atmosphere parameters, responsivities, donor scales, beam/template
response, filter coefficients, time/cadence/phase where uncertain, and any
other modeled response term.  For a differentiable conditional model,
\begin{equation}
 \Sigma_y^{\rm sys}\approx
 J_{\eta}\Sigma_{\eta}J_{\eta}^{\mathrm T},
 \qquad
 J_{\eta}=\left.\frac{\partial\E[\mathbf y\mid\Omega,\eta]}
                       {\partial\eta}\right|_{\eta_0}.
 \tag{RTC-22}\label{eq:systematic}
\end{equation}
Cross terms with sample noise are required when nuisance estimates and
timestream noise are correlated.  Nonlinear propagation or samples are
required when linearization is inadequate.  Each nuisance records value,
uncertainty, provenance, validity, and detector/array/observation/cohort/global
correlation scope.  Common terms do not average down as independent samples.

The total covariance is not advertised unless every term required by the
claim is available:
\begin{equation}
 \Sigma_y^{\rm total}=Sigma_y^{\rm stat}+\Sigma_y^{\rm sys}
   +\Sigma_y^{\rm selection}+\Sigma_y^{\rm model}
   +\hbox{required cross terms}.
 \tag{RTC-23}\label{eq:totalunc}
\end{equation}
An unavailable term remains labeled unavailable.  Conditional weights may be
used only for a consumer that explicitly accepts their exclusions; they
cannot support an absolute significance or total-error claim.

\section{Temporal response}

\subsection{Impulse, frequency, amplitude, and phase}

For a fixed, infinite-support or steady-state, uniformly sampled, single-rate
linear chain without masks, replacements, time-varying calibration, edges, or
data-derived state, the pre-decimation impulse response is
\begin{equation}
 h^{\rm comb}_d=h^{(K)}_d*\cdots*h^{(1)}_d,
 \quad
 H^{\rm comb}_d(e^{i\omega})=
   \prod_{k=1}^{K}H^{(k)}_d(e^{i\omega}).
 \tag{RTC-24}\label{eq:combinedH}
\end{equation}
The amplitude response is $|H|$, power response $|H|^2$, phase
$\phi(\omega)=\arg H$, and group delay
\begin{equation}
 \tau_g(\omega)=-\Delta t\,\frac{d}{d\omega}
                 \operatorname{unwrap}\phi(\omega),
 \tag{RTC-25}\label{eq:groupdelay}
\end{equation}
where the phase convention and undefined points at response zeros are stated.
A zero-phase forward/backward filter has different magnitude, boundary, and
effective order from a causal pass.  A notch must record center, bandwidth,
depth, sampling rate, coefficient convention, and finite-precision realized
zeros/poles.  Chained LTI filters commute only in the ideal convolution
interior; state, masking, finite windows, nonlinear detection, precision, and
edge rules generally make order observable.

For output cell $(d,n)$ and input cell $(q,j)$, the exact local response is the
Jacobian
\begin{equation}
 K_{dn,qj}=\frac{\partial y_{dn}}{\partial x^{\rm acq}_{qj}}.
 \tag{RTC-26}\label{eq:kernel}
\end{equation}
This general kernel includes alignment, detector mixing from replacement,
calibration, filter memory, and decimation.  It is a four-index sparse or
factorized operator, not necessarily a one-dimensional per-detector kernel.
For fixed selections it equals $L_{\Omega}$; at selection boundaries the
derivative may be discontinuous or undefined.  A claimed one-dimensional
kernel is valid only after proving detector separability and temporal
invariance for the stated support.

\subsection{Decimation and alias response}

For an LTI prefilter $H$ followed by equation~\eqref{eq:decimate}, the output
spectrum satisfies the multirate identity
\begin{equation}
 Y(e^{i\Omega})=\frac{1}{M}\sum_{r=0}^{M-1}
 e^{i(\Omega+2\pi r)p/M}
 H\!\left(e^{i(\Omega+2\pi r)/M}\right)
 X\!\left(e^{i(\Omega+2\pi r)/M}\right),
 \tag{RTC-27}\label{eq:alias}
\end{equation}
up to the explicitly selected Fourier/sign convention.  The $M$ folded bands
show why rate, phase, and anti-alias attenuation are part of the response.
An alias-free passband claim requires a preregistered input band and sufficient
stopband rejection in every folded image.  If unknown high-frequency content
can fold into band, the output response is conditional rather than alias-free.

\subsection{Edges, masks, replacements, and LTI existence}

For an FIR with support $\mathcal L$ and decimation point $p+nM$, the direct
input support is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal S_{n}=\{p+nM-\ell:\ell\in\mathcal L\},
 \tag{RTC-28}\label{eq:support}
\end{equation}
expanded through every preceding filter and donor/alignment mapping.  IIR
support extends to the state-reset/context boundary and, mathematically, can
be infinite.  A finite guard is an approximation whose residual tail bound
must be stated.  Output cells lacking complete required context are edge-
invalid, guarded, or conditionally available under one declared rule; they are
not ordinary interior samples.

A scalar LTI transfer function exists only when all of the following hold on
the claimed domain: uniform single-rate input; fixed coefficients and order;
fixed calibration; fixed detector-separable replacement (normally identity);
fixed mask; state independent of absolute time and data; translation-invariant
boundary treatment or an interior region isolated from edges; no selector,
clipping, normalization denominator, or coordinate mask derived from the
signal; and no rate change, or an explicitly multirate/cyclostationary
interpretation.  Otherwise the truthful response is the realized local kernel
of equation~\eqref{eq:kernel}, an affine state-space operator, or a nonlinear
input-output map.  ``Filter enabled'' is never a complete transfer identity.

Masked or replaced samples influence neighbors through the realized kernel.
That influence is intentional processing response, not new acquisition,
exposure, or independent weight.  Edge loss, mask dilation, IIR recovery, and
donor reuse must be carried into consumer eligibility and covariance.

\section{Validity, flags, weights, and provenance}

\subsection{Non-interchangeable concepts}

For each detector/sample/product, maintain distinct state for:

\begin{itemize}
\item acquisition support: original, synthesized by ALIGN, unavailable, or no
      row;
\item original value validity and detector admission;
\item numerical computability/finiteness at each RTC stage;
\item coordinate validity, coordinate frame, and valid-but-outside-product
      support;
\item descriptive flags and their causes;
\item processing masks, including source mask, spike target, and filter guard;
\item RTC replacement identity and donor influence;
\item temporal exposure/support and complete filter context;
\item science eligibility for a named consumer;
\item confidence or match quality with a defined interpretation;
\item conditional variance, conditional inverse variance, full covariance,
      response, and systematic uncertainty availability; and
\item reconstructible provenance and exact product linkage.
\end{itemize}

NaN is a numeric value state, not a missing-row or validity ontology.  A flag
does not automatically exclude a sample unless the consumer policy says so.
A mask is an operator control, not proof of bad data.  Replacement can be
finite while scientifically ineligible.  Variance is not confidence;
inverse variance is not full precision; response is not weight; zero weight
is not the same as absent exposure.

For consumer $c$, eligibility is a named predicate, for example
\begin{equation}
 E^{(c)}_{dn}=
 V^{\rm det}_d\land V^{\rm num}_{dn}\land V^{\rm coord,(c)}_{dn}
 \land V^{\rm support,(c)}_{dn}\land
 P^{(c)}(\hbox{flags,masks,replacement,response,provenance}),
 \tag{RTC-29}\label{eq:eligibility}
\end{equation}
not a universal bit.  By default, ALIGN-synthesized and RTC-replaced samples
have zero direct independent eligibility/weight while their propagated
influence remains disclosed.  Any broader admission needs a separately
approved consumer response and covariance rule.

\subsection{Finite and missing policy}

Required malformed structure, missing factor identity, invalid required
coordinate, ambiguous frame, nonfinite required coefficient/state, invalid
calibration domain, impossible donor conversion, unstable selected filter, or
unrecoverable state poisoning fails before a required product is reported.
Optional unavailable detail is labeled \unavail{} and must not alter values,
flags, eligibility, or failure behavior.  Omitted fields are distinguished
from present-invalid fields.  No sentinel value, zero, endpoint clamp, prior
observation state, or stale buffer substitutes for unavailable authority.

\subsection{Compact but exact provenance}

The standard representation may be factorized and exception-based.  It must
always permit reconstruction of:

\begin{itemize}
\item input observation, files/digests, detector keys, selected/matched APT
      lineage, and ordered column binding;
\item ALIGN grid, rate, phase, support, offsets, mapping version, exception
      intervals, and acquired, synthesized, and guarded counts;
\item each calibration factor and exclusion, atmosphere operator/domain,
      beam/template, unit, nuisance scope, and whether it was applied;
\item spike detector version, target intervals, donor links/weights,
      responsivity convention, fallback, and replacement validity;
\item every filter in chronological order, exact coefficients/digest,
      precision, normalization, state, context, mask, edge, and finite policy;
\item source-mask catalog/coordinate/frame/topology/geometry and invalidity;
\item input/output rates, $M$, $p$, block/point convention, output timestamps,
      cardinality, flag/support aggregation, and anti-alias claim;
\item factorized response/kernel availability, covariance availability,
      diagnostics/counts, and exact output product digests; and
\item requested, effective, observation-resolved, and realized records written
      atomically with no realized-to-requested backflow.
\end{itemize}

Dense per-sample provenance, response matrices, or covariance matrices are not
required routinely.  Compact run intervals, coefficient/state records, and
generative mappings are adequate only if they reproduce the exact operator and
exceptions.  Optional detail must be numerically inert.

\section{Output and provisional consumer contracts}

\subsection{RTC output bundle}

An RTC output is one atomic bundle, not an unqualified TOD array:
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal O_{\rm RTC}=
 (\mathbf y,\mathcal I,\mathcal K,\mathcal F,\mathcal V,
  \mathcal W,\mathcal P,\mathcal D),
 \tag{RTC-30}\label{eq:bundle}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal I$ is ordered sample/detector/scan/support identity,
$\mathcal K$ is the exact factorized kernel/response or truthful availability,
$\mathcal F$ typed flags and causes, $\mathcal V$ factorized validity and
eligibility inputs, $\mathcal W$ conditional variance/weight and covariance
availability, $\mathcal P$ four-stage provenance, and $\mathcal D$ diagnostics
and counts.

The TOD shape is $D_{\rm out}\times N_y$ with explicit memory and index order.
The general kernel shape is
\[
 (D_{\rm out},N_y;D_{\rm in},N),
\]
represented sparsely or factorized when possible.  Flag and status
cardinalities must be declared: detector-only,
sample-only, detector-by-sample, interval, or product-level cannot be guessed.
Diagnostics name their estimator, unit, support, mask, and validity.  Every
field states missing and nonfinite policy and whether omission means not requested,
not applicable, unavailable, or write failure.

\subsection{PTC provisional input contract}

PTC may consume only existing-use RTC output and receives no authorization to
change correlated-mode/PCA mathematics.  Before cleaning or weight formation,
it needs:

\begin{itemize}
\item exact TOD unit and calibration/beam/atmosphere status;
\item detector/column binding and stable scan/science/context identities;
\item input/output cadence, decimation phase, sample times and complete
      temporal support;
\item the realized filter order, kernel/transfer response, passband, phase,
      group delay, edge/guard state, and alias limitation;
\item original/synthesized/replaced influence, donor correlations, source
      masks, flags, numerical and detector validity, and consumer eligibility;
\item conditional variance/weight semantics and all unavailable covariance or
      response-systematic terms; and
\item exact requested/effective/resolved/realized provenance and product links.
\end{itemize}

PTC must not treat synthesized/replaced cells as independent observations,
diagonalize unknown covariance silently, infer a flat transfer function, or
use \code{sens} as an undocumented signal factor.  Unknown required response,
sample identity, or validity fails the affected cleaning/weight claim.

\subsection{VAL provisional input contract}

VAL remains limited to its existing policy and receives no package-wide policy
decision.  Eligibility evaluation needs exact cause-preserving state for:

\begin{itemize}
\item acquisition unavailable/original-invalid/ALIGN-synthesized support;
\item spike target, RTC replacement, donor identity, replacement validity and
      propagated influence;
\item source masks versus coordinate-invalid and valid-outside-product states;
\item filter edges, guards, incomplete IIR recovery, short/empty/unusable scan
      identity, and downsample footprint;
\item every nonfinite input/output and declared recovery or failure action;
\item flags before and after conditioning and the exact aggregation rule; and
\item calibration, response, provenance, conditional-weight, and uncertainty
      availability for the validation claim being made.
\end{itemize}

VAL must not collapse replacement, mask, flag, invalidity, and ineligibility
into one undocumented bit.  A finite value is not automatically eligible.  A
test pass does not authorize production or repair.

\subsection{BEAM provisional input contract}

BEAM detector-map consumers need, without reopening fitting/calibration:

\begin{itemize}
\item exact observation-local detector binding to measured Beammap geometry,
      including source-row association and any matcher validity;
\item admitted aligned coordinate frame/topology, focal-plane basis/version,
      scan/source-crossing identity, coordinate validity, and map support;
\item top-of-atmosphere calibration and per-detector beam/template identity,
      distinct from the realized RTC temporal/filter response;
\item complete RTC amplitude/phase/delay response over source-crossing
      frequencies, decimation/alias state, source-mask behavior, edge loss,
      replacement influence, and detector/scan eligibility; and
\item factor, kernel, flags, uncertainty availability, and four-stage
      provenance sufficient to reproduce the selected samples.
\end{itemize}

The calibration beam is not the filtered product response.  A filter that
attenuates or phase-shifts a crossing must expose that effect; BEAM must not
infer an unchanged PSF or peak normalization.  Missing detector association,
required coordinate validity, or response identity fails the detector-map
claim.  No new BEAM fitting, calibration, or production mode is authorized.

\subsection{Analytic limiting checks}

Required limiting checks include: $\tau\rightarrow0$ gives unity extinction;
$p\rightarrow0$ replacement gives identity; an exact donor with
$g_d\rho_d=g_q\rho_q$ gives unit donor scale; filter coefficients approaching
the Kronecker delta give identity; notch radius/width limits follow the exact
coefficient convention; $M\rightarrow1,p=0$ gives no rate change; a source
radius approaching zero selects only exact valid coincidences under the
declared boundary; complete context approaches the interior LTI response; and
a pole approaching the unit circle exposes increasing memory and never
silently retains a stability claim at or outside it.

\subsection{Standard-method disposition}

No deterministic method explicitly required by the dispatch is omitted.
Additional common methods are dispositioned as follows so absence is not
mistaken for evidence:

\begin{itemize}
\item A broadband chirp is omitted from Phase 1 because the preregistered
      impulse transform plus sinusoid grid tests identify the fixed linear
      response without sweep-rate/window ambiguity.  It may be a focused
      Phase-2 convenience, not replacement evidence.
\item Monte Carlo/white-noise transfer and empirical covariance estimation are
      omitted because no stochastic model, seed, trial count, or numerical
      study is authorized.  Equations~\eqref{eq:cov}--\eqref{eq:totalcov}
      state the exact requirement; a future costly study needs its own gate.
\item Real-observation source injection, jackknife, split-map, and end-to-end
      reductions are omitted because local Citlali reductions and broad
      campaigns are prohibited.  Static or existing focused deterministic
      tests cannot substitute for future exact-SHA evidence.
\item Empirical system identification or fitted deconvolution is omitted
      because it would estimate rather than trace the exact implemented
      operator and could hide nonlinearity, edge behavior, or factor mistakes.
\item Dense full-observation response/covariance materialization is omitted
      because the authority permits compact/factorized representations and no
      complete input covariance exists.  Bounded matrix fixtures remain
      sufficient to falsify propagation algebra.
\item Cross-validation against the three submitted handoffs is deferred by
      design until after the frozen core and coordinator approval; opening
      them now would violate independence.
\item A production performance or numerical-tolerance campaign is omitted
      because this phase makes no implementation observation and the dispatch
      authorizes no broad or costly execution.  Floating tolerances must be
      derived from the exact operation and representation before any focused
      test, never from candidate output.
\end{itemize}

and order, coefficient conventions, initial/final state, mask behavior,

The first later source opening, only after coordinator approval, is intended
to be \code{include/citlali/core/timestream/rtc/rtcproc.h}, followed only as

three post-core handoffs are opened only after their digests are reverified and
their first-open times recorded.

A mature RTC numerical internal is reopened as Tier A only if source and a
deterministic falsification establish a concrete contradiction such as:

\begin{itemize}
