\begin{abstract}
At application commit
\code{46ad23888a40f5102cdfd50c06e49a549bdf8a20}, the RTC interface is
\textbf{nonconformant}.  The implementation has useful positive controls---a
typed one-way requested, effective, observation-resolved, and realized skeleton, validation that
forbids downsampling without filtering, direct replacement flags, matched
signal and kernel application for several filter stages, and fail-propagating
provenance output---but these controls do not close the scientific contract.
Current source applies a nonconforming extinction response, omits the
calibration-factor ratio in calibrated-domain donor replacement, fails to
propagate donor, filter, and IIR influence into validity, applies an enabled alt-az
projection to signal but not kernel, fails open for invalid source-mask
coordinates, permits unguarded observation-edge FIR values in the science
profile, and does not persist enough realized identity to reconstruct the
operator or product bundle.  ALIGN, CAL, and AST remain open conditioned
dependencies.  Contract status is \status{proposed}, validation is
\status{in_progress}, production remains \status{existing_use_only}, and the
verdict proposal is \textbf{amend}.  This report authorizes no repair,
re-audit, execution, downstream package launch, or production expansion.
\end{abstract}

\section{Frozen independent contract and equations}

\subsection{Identities, states, and operator}

continuity replacements, conditioned samples, decimated cells, and named

\begin{equation}

\quad r_s\in\{0.5,1,2,4\},\quad f_{\rm ref}=122.0703125\ {\rm Hz}.
\tag{RTC-01}
\end{equation}
Requested, effective, observation-resolved, and realized state are immutable
one-way stages.  A realized record must identify the exact factors,

Let $A$ be the admitted ALIGN operator.  The factorized gain and calibrated

\begin{align}

\tag{RTC-04}\\

\tag{RTC-05}
\end{align}

\gamma_{d\leftarrow q,j}=\frac{g_{dj}\rho_d}{g_{qj}\rho_q}.
\tag{RTC-07}
\end{equation}
Conditioned on the realized selection $\omega=(p,\mathcal Q,w,\gamma,\beta)$,

\section{Exact implemented operator at the governing SHA}

\subsection{Observed stage order}

The traced call order is: optional polarization; initialize \code{fcf};
calibration; extinction; kernel construction; despike detection and donor
replacement; pre-filter fixed/data-derived notches; symmetric FIR and
configured notch; optional IIR highpass; edge-guard flagging; optional
detector-local notches; outer-RTC capture; inner crop and optional phase-zero
striding; post-decimation data-derived notches and flag-only diagnostic masks;
signal-only alt-az destriping; diagnostics; then PTC.  Standard callers add APT,
bad-detector, and duplicate-tone exclusions before RTC output/PTC use.

Conditioned on all data-derived choices, a compact representation of the
implemented inner signal path is
\begin{equation}
\mathbf y_{\rm impl}=
P_XN_{\rm post}D_{0,M}S N_{\rm det}Q_{\rm HP}N_{\rm cfg}W N_{\rm pre}
R_{\widehat\omega}C_{\rm impl}A\mathbf x+\mathbf a_{\rm impl}.
\tag{IMP-01}
\end{equation}
Here $S$ selects the inner scan, $D_{0,M}$ is first-cell striding,
$N_{\rm pre},N_{\rm det},N_{\rm post}$ can be data-derived notch cascades,
$W$ is the FIR, $Q_{\rm HP}$ the highpass, and $P_X$ is the flag-conditioned
alt-az template projection.  Flag/mask operations $\Phi_{\rm impl}$ evolve in
parallel but are not the causal-support operator for (IMP-01).

The kernel path is approximately
\begin{equation}
\mathbf k_{\rm impl}=
N_{\rm post}D_{0,M}S N_{\rm det}Q_{\rm HP}N_{\rm cfg}W N_{\rm pre}\mathbf k_0,
\tag{IMP-02}
\end{equation}
subject to which optional notch path was realized.  It omits
$R_{\widehat\omega}$ donor mixing and $P_X$.  That is a concrete response
contradiction and a named Tier-A reopen trigger limited to the interface and
response: the audit does not redesign the mature algorithms.

The outer RTC product stops after $N_{\rm det}$ and before $S,D_{0,M},
N_{\rm post}$, post-filter masks, and $P_X$.  Therefore ``RTC'' without an
explicit stage identity is ambiguous.

\subsection{Calibration and replacement equations}

The implemented detector multiplier before replacement is
\begin{equation}
g^{\rm impl}_{dj}=f^{\rm conv}_d f^{\rm flxscale}_d
\exp(\widehat\tau_{b(d),j}),
\tag{IMP-03}
\end{equation}
for the non-q0 path.  Source first derives a band opacity that is
zenith-equivalent by division by airmass, then applies $\exp(\widehat\tau_b)$
rather than the required sample line-of-sight factor.  The q0 path returns zero
band opacity and hence unity correction.  No complete strict domain/factor
identity gate is present.  \code{fcf} stores \code{flux_conversion_factor},
then its mean extinction multiplier, but not \code{flxscale}; it is not the
complete factor in (IMP-03).

Replacement is executed after (IMP-03).  For each flagged target region,
eligible donor residuals are divided by donor \code{responsivity}, averaged,
multiplied by target \code{responsivity}, then added to a target linear
baseline and a deterministic Gaussian-like fill.  Thus the multiplicative
part is $\rho_d/\rho_q$, while RTC-07 requires
$g_{dj}\rho_d/(g_{qj}\rho_q)$.  Donor responsivity and donor-region finiteness
are checked; target responsivity is not.  No donor yields the baseline.  Every
replaced target remains directly flagged, a positive control that does not
prevent its value from entering later filters and influencing unflagged rows.

\subsection{Filters, state, edges, and decimation}

The FIR is a symmetric Kaiser-like $2n_{\rm terms}+1$ coefficient vector.  Its
sum-normalization code is commented out.  A deterministic source-formula probe
at $f_s=122.0703125$ Hz, $a_{\rm Gibbs}=50$, $n_{\rm terms}=32$ produced sums
0.9995720037513057, 1.000116339580343, 0.9993723986695066, and
1.0006199825360638 for 16, 20, 30, and 32 Hz respectively.  These are exact
formula facts for the probe, not a compiled or astronomical bias claim.

Only the valid-convolution interior overwrites input; the first and last
$n_{\rm terms}$ rows retain their original values.  Outer context usually
supplies interior support, but absent observation-boundary context is only
flagged when the optional flag-mode edge guard is enabled.  Current science
configuration enables a 32-term FIR and omits the RTC edge-guard block; Point,
OOF, and Beammap explicitly enable it.

Configured and data-derived notches use sequential biquad stages; the usual
path is forward/reverse with odd extension.  The optional highpass is a
repeated first-order recurrence with selected direction and zero initial state.
These operations consume payloads without a typed validity mask or finite
policy.  A global stationary transfer therefore does not exist across
selection, flags, boundaries, and data-derived notches; only a conditioned
local operator can be claimed.

Downsampling selects rows $0,M,2M,\ldots$ and has
$N_y=\lceil N/M\rceil$.  Telescope fields, pointing, angle, signal, and kernel
use the same phase-zero stride.  Flags are OR-reduced over each adjacent
$M$-cell block.  Typed validation positively forbids enabled downsampling
without filtering and rejects \code{freq_high_Hz} above the output Nyquist.
It does not demonstrate stopband attenuation or propagate the wider FIR/IIR
and donor support into flags.  Output representative-time semantics remain
implicit first-cell sampling.

\subsection{Source mask and alt-az projection}

Source protection is enabled only for the resolved pointing reduction path.
The mask computes detector tangent position and marks a radius about map
center.  Unsupported/empty coordinates and nonpositive or non-finite radius
produce an all-false mask; non-finite coordinate rows are skipped.  A supported
mode sets \code{info.valid=true} independent of complete coordinate validity,
and RTC ignores this status.  Invalid coordinate therefore becomes outside or
unprotected, contrary to the fail-closed RTC-13/RTC-29 boundary.

Alt-az destriping builds time, azimuth, elevation, and optional derivative
templates; selects rows using signal flags and finite telescope templates;
solves detector-local least squares; and subtracts $X\widehat\beta$ from signal
only.  For fixed good rows its linear response is a projection
$P_X=I-X(X_G^{\rm T}X_G)^{-1}X_G^{\rm T}$ with the appropriate row-selection
embedding.  Kernel is never transformed by $P_X$.  Both current science and
Beammap configurations enable alt-az destriping and kernel generation, so this
is exercised by declared current profiles rather than a dormant contradiction.
