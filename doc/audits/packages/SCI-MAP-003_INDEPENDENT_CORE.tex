\section{Scientific claim and conditionality}

For one exact OOF/JINC product cell, let an exact discrete reference tracer
$g$ and the final normalized response kernel $k$ live on the same admitted map
grid.  The proposed transformation is
\[
 (g,k,\mathcal I) \longrightarrow (\widehat H,\mathcal D,\mathcal I_H)
 \longrightarrow P_{\mathrm{pred}}(\theta),
\]
where $\mathcal I$ is immutable observation/reduction/product identity,
$\mathcal D$ is an admitted Fourier-domain set, and $\mathcal I_H$ is the
persisted transfer identity.  This is system identification from a known
tracer and its final response.  It is not deconvolution of an observed OOF map.

The independent result is conditional:

\begin{quote}
If $g$ and $k$ are finite, identity-compatible realizations of the same exact
linear, shift-invariant working-grid experiment, and if a denominator domain
and all grid operators are approved, then the frequency-by-frequency
least-squares transfer is $\widehat H=G^*K/|G|^2$ on that domain.  The admitted
authority does not yet identify the exact $g$, terminal $k$ parent, validity
eligibility, denominator policy, padding/windowing operator, uncertainty
product, persistence schema, or LMTOOF association.  Therefore no actual
transfer product or LMTOOF consumer is presently available.
\end{quote}

The ordinary claim is only an effective map-center, point-source,
linearized response for one exact observation/reduction, band, mode,
configuration, detector/mask selection, realization, map slot, and terminal
iteration/pass.  It is neither a universal instrument transfer nor a claim
about off-center, spatially varying, extended, arbitrary-morphology, nonlinear,
cross-band, cross-mode, or cross-iteration behavior.

\section{Exact mathematical objects}

\subsection{Spatial grid, axes, frame, and indexing}

Let the admitted parent plane have shape $N_y\times N_x$, with memory indices
\[
 r\in\{0,\ldots,N_y-1\},\qquad c\in\{0,\ldots,N_x-1\}.
\]
The first array dimension is the row/elevation-like dimension and the second
is the column/azimuth-like dimension.  For the OOF parent, FITS axis 1 is
\texttt{AZOFFSET}, FITS axis 2 is \texttt{ELOFFSET}, and both spatial world
axes are AltAz tangent-plane offsets in arcseconds about the admitted map
center.  FITS/WCS pixel coordinates are one-based; the in-memory $(r,c)$
indices and Fourier indices below are zero-based.  Axis sign, orientation,
handedness, pixel scale, epoch/frame, and center come from the full-precision
parent WCS, never from array position or a nominal plotting convention.

Let $A$ be the full-precision local $2\times2$ linear WCS map, in
arcseconds per pixel, from column/row displacement to
azimuth/elevation tangent-plane displacement.  If
$c_0=\mathrm{CRPIX1}-1$ and $r_0=\mathrm{CRPIX2}-1$, then locally
\[
 \begin{bmatrix}x_{r,c}\\y_{r,c}\end{bmatrix}
 = A\begin{bmatrix}c-c_0\\r-r_0\end{bmatrix}.
\]
A singular, non-finite, inconsistent, identity-mismatched, or out-of-domain
WCS makes transfer construction unavailable.  A fractional reference pixel
does not authorize resampling or independent peak recentering.

The exact parent shape, WCS, map slot, Stokes identity (the validated lane is
Stokes I, typed index 0), array/band identity, and signal/kernel unit must be
identical for $g$ and $k$.  General reprojection, interpolation, fractional
alignment, or implicit recentering is not admitted.

\subsection{Reference, response, and Fourier objects}

The spatial objects are
\[
 g,k\in\R^{N_y\times N_x}.
\]
Both carry map-signal/kernel unit \texttt{mJy/beam} under the admitted current
product convention.  Their physical pixel values must be finite on the exact
rectangular working support presented to the transform.  A mask, missing
pixel, NaN, or infinite value cannot be silently replaced by zero.  Any
masking, inpainting, zero-fill, window, crop, or padding is a separately named
linear operator and part of product identity.

The reference $g$ is the exact discrete APT-Gaussian tracer after the selected
APT rows/detector mixture, amplitude, truncation, centering, pixelization, and
every admitted response-changing preparation.  Those selections are not yet
specified by authority (nondecision ND-G).  The response $k$ is the exact final
normalized JINC $K/C$ plane: the realized processing-filtered source-template
response projected through JINC and normalized by the same formal support
denominator.  It must not be convolved through JINC or globally normalized a
second time.  It is not relabeled as an analytic beam or measured PSF.

For mathematical reference this core uses the unshifted, forward-unnormalized
two-dimensional DFT pair
\begin{align}
 X_{p,q}=\FFT[x]_{p,q}
 &=\sum_{r=0}^{N_y-1}\sum_{c=0}^{N_x-1}
 x_{r,c}\exp\!\left[-2\pi i\left(\frac{pr}{N_y}+\frac{qc}{N_x}\right)\right],\\
 x_{r,c}=\IFFT[X]_{r,c}
 &=\frac{1}{N_xN_y}\sum_{p=0}^{N_y-1}\sum_{q=0}^{N_x-1}
 X_{p,q}\exp\!\left[+2\pi i\left(\frac{pr}{N_y}+\frac{qc}{N_x}\right)\right].
\end{align}
Thus $G=\FFT[g]$ and $K=\FFT[k]$ have identical transform-dependent units,
and their quotient $H$ is dimensionless.  A different matched normalization
is mathematically equivalent for $H$ but must be named for persistence and
forward synthesis.  \texttt{fftshift} is display/storage ordering, not a new
scientific axis; if used it must be applied consistently and recorded.

Define wrapped signed frequency indices
\[
 \widetilde q=\begin{cases}q,&q\leq\lfloor N_x/2\rfloor,\\q-N_x,&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}
 \quad
 \widetilde p=\begin{cases}p,&p\leq\lfloor N_y/2\rfloor,\\p-N_y,&\text{otherwise}.
 \end{cases}
\]
Nyquist-bin sign at even length is a storage convention that must be frozen;
the conjugate-pair identity is authoritative.  The corresponding world
spatial-frequency vector is
\[
 \boldsymbol\nu_{p,q}=A^{-T}
 \begin{bmatrix}\widetilde q/N_x\\\widetilde p/N_y\end{bmatrix}
 \quad\text{cycles per arcsecond}.
\]
This dual-WCS construction preserves axis sign, orientation, handedness, and
pixel scale.  A radial coordinate $\|\boldsymbol\nu\|$ may be diagnostic, but
radialization is not the product.

Using one common array origin for $g$ and $k$ preserves relative phase.
Changing to a common centered-coordinate Fourier convention multiplies both
transforms by the same phase and cancels in $K/G$.  Recentring $g$ and $k$
independently changes their relative phase and is forbidden.  Padding,
cropping, apodization, and boundary conditions do not generally cancel and
remain nondecision ND-GRID.

\subsection{Estimator and admitted denominator domain}

On the exact working grid, the effective model is
\[
 k=h\circledast g+e,
 \qquad K_{p,q}=H^{\mathrm{true}}_{p,q}G_{p,q}+E_{p,q},
\]
where $\circledast$ is circular convolution under the stated DFT.  A claim of
linear rather than circular convolution requires an explicit zero-padding and
crop operator.  The residual $e$ includes stochastic error and model
discrepancy; the equation is an approximation limited to the admitted
map-center response.

Let $d_{p,q}=|G_{p,q}|^2$.  The denominator domain must be a versioned set
\[
 \mathcal D=\{(p,q): I_{p,q}=1,\ G_{p,q},K_{p,q},d_{p,q}\text{ finite},
 \ d_{p,q}\ \bowtie\ \tau_{p,q},\ B_{p,q}=1\},
\]
where $I$ is input/identity validity, $B$ is an independently declared
frequency-band restriction, $\tau$ is an exact threshold reference, and
$\bowtie$ is the frozen boundary comparator.  None of $\tau$, $B$, tapering,
or the at-threshold comparator is approved by the admitted authority
(nondecision ND-DEN).  Zero denominator is always excluded.

For $(p,q)\in\mathcal D$, the unregularized one-regressor complex
least-squares estimator is
\begin{equation}
 \widehat H_{p,q}
 =\frac{G_{p,q}^{*}K_{p,q}}{|G_{p,q}|^2}
 =\frac{K_{p,q}}{G_{p,q}}.
 \label{eq:estimator}
\end{equation}
Outside $\mathcal D$, $H$ is missing/invalid, not numerically zero.  A
Tikhonov/Wiener denominator, clipping, extrapolation, interpolation, or
high-frequency fill is a different estimator and is not authorized.  A taper
$T_{p,q}$ is a distinct response factor and must be persisted rather than
hidden in $H$.

The product is the pair $(\widehat H,\mathcal D)$ plus exact identity, not a
bare complex array.  Because inverse transformation needs a complete spectrum,
LMTOOF also requires an approved synthesis operator $S_{\mathcal D}$ that
defines excluded frequencies and any taper.  No such operator is approved;
therefore the forward consumer remains unavailable even if values on
$\mathcal D$ can be computed.

\subsection{Forward-model operator}

For an approved consumer and an optical prediction
$P_{\mathrm{opt}}(\theta)$ on the identical working grid and in a declared
compatible amplitude unit,
\begin{equation}
 P_{\mathrm{pred}}(\theta)=
 \IFFT\!\left[S_{\mathcal D}(\widehat H)\,
 \FFT\!\left(P_{\mathrm{opt}}(\theta)\right)\right].
 \label{eq:forward}
\end{equation}
The FFT convention, grid, padding/crop/window operator, axes, and parent
identity must match those of $H$.  $H$ is constructed once per exact OOF
observation/reduction and is fixed for every trial $\theta$ within that one
LMTOOF solve.  Trial aberrations are never transfer-construction inputs.  A
later telescope correction requires a later OOF observation/reduction and a
new transfer identity; $H$ is not updated inside a solve.

If $P_{\mathrm{opt}}$ is in \texttt{mJy/beam}, the predicted plane retains
that unit because $H$ is dimensionless.  If the optical model is normalized or
dimensionless, its conversion and any nuisance amplitude are a separately
fitted consumer parameter.  Authority has not selected that amplitude
contract (ND-AMP).

\section{Expectation and uncertainty contract}

\subsection{Conditionally exact reference}

If $G$ is treated as fixed and
$K=H^{\mathrm{true}}G+E$, then on $\mathcal D$
\[
 \E[\widehat H_{j}\mid G,\mathcal D]
 =H^{\mathrm{true}}_{j}+\frac{G_j^*}{|G_j|^2}\E[E_j\mid G,\mathcal D].
\]
The estimator is conditionally unbiased only if the residual is conditionally
zero mean and domain selection introduces no unmodeled selection effect.  With
Hermitian complex covariance
\[
 C_K(j,\ell)=\E[(K_j-\E K_j)(K_\ell-\E K_\ell)^*],
\]
the full conditional covariance is
\begin{equation}
 C_H(j,\ell)=
 \frac{G_j^*}{|G_j|^2}\,C_K(j,\ell)\,
 \frac{G_\ell}{|G_\ell|^2},
 \qquad
 \Var(\widehat H_j\mid G)=\frac{C_K(j,j)}{|G_j|^2}.
 \label{eq:cov-fixed-g}
\end{equation}
For a general complex process, the pseudocovariance
$P_H(j,\ell)=a_jP_K(j,\ell)a_\ell$, with
$a_j=G_j^*/|G_j|^2$, is also required.  Per-frequency variances do not replace
off-diagonal covariance and do not establish independence.

\subsection{Uncertain reference and joint covariance}

If both $G$ and $K$ are uncertain, the quotient is nonlinear and is generally
biased.  First-order perturbation gives
\[
 \delta H_j\simeq \frac{\delta K_j}{G_j}
 -\frac{K_j}{G_j^2}\delta G_j.
\]
Let $z=(K,G)$ and
$J_j=(1/G_j,-K_j/G_j^2)$.  To first order,
\[
 C_H(j,\ell)\simeq J_j\,C_z(j,\ell)\,J_\ell^\dagger,
\]
with the corresponding pseudocovariance and all $G$--$K$ cross terms.
Near the denominator boundary this linearization may fail; threshold selection
itself is then random and requires a selection-aware method or exclusion.

No admitted authority supplies $C_K$, $C_G$, their cross-covariance,
pseudocovariance, a sampling distribution, repeat-observation empirical
distribution, or a calibration/systematic error model.  Consequently:
\begin{itemize}
\item formal variance and full covariance are mathematically defined above but
numerically \NA;
\item empirical uncertainty is \NA, because no authorized repeat or synthetic
execution exists;
\item calibration/systematic uncertainty from APT amplitude/mixture, map
calibration, WCS, masks, RTC/PTC/JINC response, and terminal iteration is
\NA;
\item response uncertainty is \NA; $H$ must not be advertised as exact merely
because the quotient is deterministic for realized inputs; and
\item confidence intervals, inverse-variance weighting, likelihood weights,
and LMTOOF uncertainty propagation are unauthorized.
\end{itemize}

For fixed $P_{\mathrm{opt}}$, prediction covariance would be the linear
propagation of $C_H$ through Equation~\ref{eq:forward}.  Optical-model and
fitted-parameter covariance are separate consumer-owned terms.  Because neither
$C_H$ nor an approved synthesis operator exists, prediction covariance is
currently \NA.

\section{Configuration and realization lifecycle}

The one-way lifecycle is:
\[
 \text{requested}\rightarrow\text{effective}\rightarrow
 \text{observation-resolved}\rightarrow\text{realized}.
\]

\begin{description}
\item[Requested.] Whether a transfer is requested; requested publication and
consumer intent; requested RTC/PTC/JINC, map, padding/window, denominator, and
uncertainty policies.  Disabled expert values remain requested facts, not
executed behavior.
\item[Effective.] Context-free activation, required/optional publication,
representation, DFT normalization/order, grid operator, denominator algorithm,
comparator, taper/synthesis rule, and failure policy after validation.
\item[Observation-resolved.] Exact observation/reduction, array/band/mode/map
slot, APT rows and detector mixture, calibration, masks, WCS/shape, parent
map/kernel, sample-dependent RTC/PTC/JINC state, threshold values, and selected
terminal iteration/pass.
\item[Realized.] Exact $g$ and $k$ bytes/digests, transforms, denominator
values, admitted-domain mask/count, response values, status, excluded-frequency
reasons, uncertainty availability, persisted product digest, and completion or
failure state.
\end{description}

Later stages never rewrite earlier authority.  Provenance reports state; it is
not an execution control path.  A later observation after telescope correction
creates a new realization and transfer identity.
