noise-product implementation computes a per-pixel finite-stack mean and the
empirically centered second central moment with divisor (R), then uses the

adaptive feedback, aperture uncertainty, or scientifically adequate
production count.  The verdict is \textbf{amend}; current operation remains
\code{existing_use_only}, and no production count or default is selected.

\section{Accepted independent contract}

Let (Y^{(r)}\in\mathbb R^P) be completed realization (r), projected onto a
common parent, unit, response, support, and validity domain.  Let (R) be the
completed count and

\begin{align}
 \bar Y &= \frac1R\sum_{r=1}^{R}Y^{(r)},\\
 S_R &= \sum_{r=1}^{R}(Y^{(r)}-\bar Y)
                    (Y^{(r)}-\bar Y)^{\mathsf T}.
 \label{eq:audit-scatter}
\end{align}

The frozen core proves that, for arbitrary position means \(\mu_r\) and joint
design covariance \(\Gamma_{rs}\),

\begin{align}
 \E[S_R]={}&\sum_r\Gamma_{rr}
 -\frac1R\sum_{r,s}\Gamma_{rs}
 +\sum_r(\mu_r-\bar\mu)(\mu_r-\bar\mu)^{\mathsf T}.
 \label{eq:audit-general-expectation}
\end{align}

No divisor follows from (R) alone.  Only in the iid, common-mean case is
(S_R/(R-1)) the ordinary unbiased covariance; (S_R/R) is then low by
((R-1)/R).  Cross-realization dependence, complement or duplicate structure,
missing completed identities, source imprint, and changing support require
their own target and design treatment.

For any fixed scalar consumer (a=\ell^{\mathsf T}D), the required variance is
(\ell^{\mathsf T}T\ell), not an arbitrary map diagonal or a coefficient with
inverse-squared units.  For a fixed aperture/background operator (a),

\begin{equation}
 \Var(a^{\mathsf T}m)=a^{\mathsf T}Ta,
 \label{eq:audit-aperture}
\end{equation}

including off-diagonal terms.  Significance additionally requires the null law
of the complete statistic, search, selection, and multiplicity.  Adaptive
feedback additionally requires the joint update/stopping law.  These
requirements are not promoted by a count, a scalar calibration, or a passing
consumer.

\section{Exact implementation estimator}

For pixel (p), exact d501 computes, with the reachable default
\code{mean_subtract=true},

\begin{align}
 \bar Y_p^{\rm d501} &= \frac1R\sum_{r=1}^{R}Y_{rp},\\
 V_p^{\rm d501} &= \frac1R\sum_{r=1}^{R}Y_{rp}^{2}
                  -(\bar Y_p^{\rm d501})^2
                =\frac{(S_R)_{pp}}{R}.
 \label{eq:d501-variance}
\end{align}

Negative roundoff is clamped to zero.  No off-diagonal covariance is formed.
Consequently, under the special iid common-law case,

\begin{equation}
 \E[V_p^{\rm d501}]=\frac{R-1}{R}K_{pp};
 \label{eq:d501-iid-bias}
\end{equation}

under a general joint assignment design its expectation is the (p,p)
element of Equation~\eqref{eq:audit-general-expectation} divided by (R).
The code applies no design correction and persists neither the joint design
nor completed realization identities needed to select one.  (R=1) returns
zero centered scatter, leaves the calibration region empty, retains scale one,
and emits zero direct-ratio values wherever its denominator is zero.  That is
a degenerate descriptive result, not an uncertainty estimate.

Let (q_p) be the snapshot of the admitted formal MAP coefficient.  The MAP
contract classifies (q_p) as a nonprecision gridding/normalization
coefficient.  Exact d501 defines a calibration region (D) using finite,
positive (q_p), a coverage threshold, and finite positive (V_p^{\rm d501}),
then computes

\begin{align}
 m &= \operatorname{median}_{p\in D}(q_pV_p^{\rm d501}),\\
 \alpha &= m^{-1},\\
 w_p^{\rm emp,d501} &= \alpha q_p.
 \label{eq:d501-scale}
\end{align}

When (D) is empty, \(\alpha\) silently remains one.  Otherwise this same-stack
operation enforces a median scale relation by construction.  It does not
establish that (q_p\) has the spatial shape of an inverse marginal variance,
cannot repair an incorrect correlation shape, and is not an external
calibration to a repeated-physical-noise target.  When requested, the scaled
coefficient overwrites the live coefficient and downstream science products
are refreshed.  Filter \code{normalize_errors} can force that application even
when the noise-product apply flag alone is false.

The other direct products are

\begin{align}
 Z_p^{q} &= D_p\sqrt{\alpha q_p},\\
 \sigma_p^{V} &= \sqrt{V_p^{\rm d501}},\\
 Z_p^{V} &=
 \begin{cases}D_p/\sigma_p^{V},&\sigma_p^{V}>0,\\0,&\text{otherwise.}\end{cases}
 \label{eq:d501-snr-family}
\end{align}

The two standardized maps agree only if (q_pV_p^{\rm d501}) is spatially
constant after scaling.  A separate \code{noise_s2n_sigma} pools
(Y_{rp}\sqrt{q_p}) over both pixels and realizations and takes an ordinary
sample standard deviation, without modeling their dependence.  These are four
different statistics.  None has a source-derived null, response, selection,
search, multiplicity, or tail calibration.
