\section{Method and evidence limits}

The review first matched all eight manifest source blob digests, then read
those exact objects.  Additional paths were opened only when a numbered core
clause required the direct association, ALIGN, AST, ENG-STATE, VAL, or product
consumer trace.  Fixture inspection used read-only NetCDF access with masking
and scaling disabled.  No fixture, executable helper, schema, wrapper,
verifier, reduction, build, test, external request, or data product was
created.

The two existing raw/recomputed pairs can demonstrate their own structure and
typed logical bytes.  They cannot prove general selection, cache safety,
atomicity, retry behavior, provenance, completion, same-row derivation, or
physical event semantics.  No authorized cadence/gap tolerance was supplied,
so interval extrema are recorded but \code{gap} is not inferred.

\section{Owner decision brief and restrictions}

The recommended owner disposition is \code{amend}: preserve the independent
core; authorize a separately scoped repair only after deciding repository
ownership and obtaining explicit TolTECA maintainer opt-in for any TolTECA
change; then require an independent re-audit against exact successor commits.
The repair scope must keep selection/identity, atomic completion, row/preserve
checks, transactional Citlali admission, and typed validity explicit.  Any
decision about coordinate formula, alias remedy semantics, interpolation
response, event meaning, timing offset, or physical row displacement is Tier A
and requires separate owner authority.

This audit performed no application, test, build, configuration, validation,
production, TolTECA, or canonical coordination edit; no Unity access, local
reduction, external contact, delegation, push, merge, repair, re-audit, or
downstream launch; and no new fixture or data production.  The package stops
for coordinator and owner review.
