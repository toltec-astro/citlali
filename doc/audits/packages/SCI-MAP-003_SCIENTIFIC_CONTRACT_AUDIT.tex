\section{Frozen mathematical contract used for comparison}

Let $J_{ai}$ be the realized JINC deposition coefficient from eligible sample
$i$ into map cell $a$, $d_i$ the signal timestream, $k_i$ the propagated
source-kernel timestream, $q_i$ the admitted per-sample scalar weight, and
$c_i$ the coefficient used by the current mapper.  The source trace implements
the serial accumulators
\[
 S_a=\sum_i q_i c_i d_i J_{ai},\qquad
 C_a=\sum_i q_i c_i J_{ai},\qquad
 Q_a=\sum_i q_i c_i^2 J_{ai},\qquad
 K^{\rm num}_a=\sum_i q_i c_i k_i J_{ai},
\]
followed, where its local support predicates pass, by
\[
 m_a=S_a/C_a,\qquad k^{\rm final}_a=K^{\rm num}_a/C_a,\qquad
 w^{\rm formal}_a=C_a^2/Q_a .
\]
These equations describe the inspected implementation; they do not promote
$q$, $c$, coverage, or $w^{\rm formal}$ to a validated precision or
covariance.  They also do not establish that $k^{\rm final}$ is the complete
terminal response.

The successor core then requires, on a common retained odd grid and WCS,
\[
 g,k\in\mathbb{R}^{N_y\times N_x},\quad N_y,N_x\ {\rm odd},\qquad
 G=\operatorname{FFT}(g),\quad K=\operatorname{FFT}(k),
\]
\[
 \widehat H_{p,q}=\frac{G_{p,q}^{*}K_{p,q}}{|G_{p,q}|^2}
                 =\frac{K_{p,q}}{G_{p,q}}\quad ((p,q)\in\mathcal D),
\]
with explicit missing/invalid state outside $\mathcal D$.  Its numbered
equations are: forward DFT (successor Eq.~1), inverse DFT (Eq.~2), complex
quotient estimator \code{eq:estimator} (Eq.~3), consumer forward operator
\code{eq:forward} (Eq.~4), and fixed-reference covariance
\code{eq:cov-fixed-g} (Eq.~5).  The exact odd crop, dual WCS, full identity,
domain, status, synthesis operator, and uncertainty rules surrounding those
equations are inseparable parts of the claim.
