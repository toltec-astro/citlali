## Scientific-owner answer

The six-package architecture has a coherent **acyclic topology** and preserves
many essential scientific separations: stable identities do not collapse into
row positions; the ALIGN-grid and RTC-grid AST roles form a two-stage diamond
rather than a cycle; RTC→CAL→PTC ordering is interpretable; producer fact,
use-owner policy, Registry binding, VAL evaluation, and consumer action remain
distinct; support, exposure, weight, response, uncertainty, and final validity
are not aliases.

The source state does **not**, however, provide a completely coherent,
source-closed, freezeable end-to-end contract chain. Two frozen PTC equations
are normatively inconsistent, required VAL bindings/profiles are unavailable,
two coordinate-boundary bodies are absent, several exact external producers are
outside the admitted packet, and MAP lacks independently necessary coefficient,
exposure, projection, and support-policy authority. The honest result is
therefore outcome B: a finite set of repairs, registrations, external
authorities, and owner decisions is required.

A non-authoritative logical `B_MAP` draft can be written—and is supplied in
`MAP_HANDOFF_PROFILE.md`—because every unavailable member is typed and every
prohibited inference is explicit. It cannot be frozen or registered as an
executable profile at this source state. **No current numerical MAP route is
available.**

## MAP and downstream disposition

The exact logical handoff bundle is sufficiently specified to support review:
sample signal, identity/parents, AST RTC-grid coordinate/WCS, MAP request or
exact `G_{pi}` parent relation, analysis coefficient, VAL decision,
support/exposure, causes/influence, calibration/beam, response/null space,
uncertainty/covariance availability, lifecycle/provenance, and
availability/failure state are all separately owned and typed.

At the pinned commit, multiple required members are unavailable. MAP must not
reconstruct a coordinate, choose a projection default, infer `omega_i` from a
loading or inverse-square unit, derive exposure from duration/hits/weight,
promote package validity to eligibility, convert eligibility to contribution,
infer response from `mJy/beam`, treat normalization as variance, set omitted
uncertainty to zero, or authorize a direct CAL bypass.

NOI, FLT, BEAM, SRC/Pointing/OOF, and FRUIT were not inspected. Their generic
envelope in `MAP_HANDOFF_PROFILE.md` is a dependency statement only and makes
no claim that any such contract accepts the bundle.
