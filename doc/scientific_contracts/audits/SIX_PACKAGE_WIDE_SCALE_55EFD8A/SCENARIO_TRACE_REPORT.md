## Reading rules

“Exists” below means that the cited contract permits or requires a numerical product under the stated scenario facts. It is not an implementation-conformity finding. “Unavailable” is never zero, identity response, zero uncertainty, or a successfully written product.

VAL axes are written in the fixed order `request / applicability / eligibility / realization`:

- `requested / applicable / eligible|ineligible / realized` is a completed decision under a usable exact profile;
- `requested / applicability_unknown / decision_unavailable / realized` is successful accounting of an unresolved registry or structural gate;
- `not_requested / — / ∅E / not_produced` asserts no eligibility proposition; and
- a failed or incomplete VAL artifact has no authoritative eligibility assertion, even if an internal calculation reached a tentative disposition.

These meanings come from [VAL-EQ::Disposition function and disposition table] and [VAL-REQ::SCI-VAL-REQ-010--SCI-VAL-REQ-015]. Product realization in the package lane remains separate from VAL decision-record realization.
