```text

       -> SCI-AST ALIGN-grid coordinates
```

External runtime roles enter only in the forward direction:

- Tune/readout supplies the exact mapping record and paired occurrence.
- Telescope/observing-state and pointing producers supply occurrence-local fields and selected correction support.

## Distinction and invariant ledger

The following invariants are jointly sufficient for `TS-A` and the closed lower-tier routes.

1. **Native order:** `I,Q` are transformed by Tune/readout before ALIGN. ALIGN acts on paired native `x/r`; RTC begins at the aligned pair and never reapplies either upstream transform.
2. **Paired identity:** `x` and `r` share one occurrence/source relation and origin state while preserving coordinate-specific numerical validity and payload availability.
3. **Stable identities:** ALIGN slot `(observation,s)`, local storage row `j`, RTC output `(observation,d,n)`, detector occurrence, and map pixel are not aliases.
4. **Exact-copy witness:** an original paired occurrence at exact ALIGN coincidence is one-hot, preserves origin, and supplies a valid ordinary witness without continuity synthesis.
5. **Clock discipline:** the reference interface, epoch relation, offset sign/unit/stage, round-half-up slot assignment, residual, and exactly-once offset count are explicit. Drift is not inferred.
6. **Topology discipline:** continuous, circular, step/state, and exact-only fields have different operators. Circular ambiguity at an exact antipode is unavailable.
7. **Cause separation:** occurrence identity, signal-coordinate validity, ALIGN mapping validity, AST coordinate validity, and use-specific eligibility remain five distinct facts.
8. **Signal/coordinate separation:** a coordinate may remain valid when `x` or `r` is unavailable; a valid coordinate does not validate signal or grant use permission.
9. **RTC state discipline:** application context, learned evidence, resolved plan, apply state, and realized record are immutable one-way stages. Apply does not relearn.
10. **RTC identity route:** `M=1`, pass-through without despiking, and an explicitly selected identity/no-filter chain are admitted. They are an explicit plan, not a default inferred from absence.
11. **Phase-zero anchor:** RTC sample `n` is anchored to selected input `M n` and time `t[M n]`. Filter delay belongs to response state and is not an automatic pointing shift.
12. **Logical completion:** complete RTC content may be produced incrementally. Chunk boundaries are engineering partitions and do not redefine scientific support or mandate materialization.
13. **CAL support:** invalid/unsupported occurrences are removed from the calibrated mathematical domain, not multiplied by zero.
14. **CAL factorization:** selected child `flxscale`, target atmosphere, and unit identity each occur exactly once. Parent or already embodied factors have runtime count zero.
15. **Atmosphere semantics:** same-observation exact/bracketed WVR interpolation is binary64 and fail-closed; no extrapolation, endpoint hold, header value, climatology, default, or another-observation substitution is authorized.
16. **Quality/support separation:** one observation-wide opacity class neither restores sample-local numerical support nor erases independently supported sample results.
17. **PTC group discipline:** network and array grouping are mutually exclusive route choices; every fit, mask, rank, operator, response, failure, and provenance fact is group-local.
18. **PTC rank discipline:** rank is explicitly integer and positive; zero, noninteger, or unrealizable rank fails without clipping, centering-only output, terminal-route conversion, or MAP fallback.
19. **PTC operator discipline:** the ordinary detector-right application recomputes mask-aware coefficients and requires the finite time-local normal matrix to have full configured rank under frozen tolerance.
20. **PTC centering:** the ordinary location is the support-normalized arithmetic mean over finite basis-fit-admitted occurrences, with binary influence, immutable segment/group scope, identity scaling, and no location restoration.
21. **Use separation:** basis fit, loading fit, operator application, output retention, and response companion are five distinct owner-bound propositions. Permission for one grants none of the others.
22. **Classification preservation:** `engineering_only` is a CAL producer fact. It neither globally prohibits PTC mathematics nor creates CAL science qualification.
23. **Response separation:** signal realization and response availability are independent. A fixed-state derivative does not represent movement of learned/selected state.
24. **Uncertainty separation:** conditional/statistical covariance, nuisance/systematic uncertainty, selection uncertainty, response uncertainty, and total uncertainty are not aliases; missing terms are unavailable, not zero or independent.
25. **Exposure immutability:** `e_acq` and `e_vo` remain attached to stable original occurrences. Synthesis, replacement, filtering, decimation, calibration, PTC transformation, weights, or repeated support creates no new acquisition.
26. **Reference-first lineage:** each stage passes the content it owns and retains resolvable immutable parent references. It does not copy or reinterpret the complete upstream history.
27. **MAP firewall:** continuous AST coordinates are allowed; deposition, map support, map response, map weighting, coaddition, reprojection, and mapping validity are unavailable and unexamined.
28. **Claim separation:** contract correctness does not establish implementation, representation fidelity, observational performance, qualification, readiness, or production status.

Disposition: **closed at contract-architecture level**.

### Independent finding 2 — complete source/beam response seed is absent

Disposition: **blocking for `TS-R`**.

The packet supplies:

- exact ALIGN and RTC conditional response semantics;
- CAL multiplier/companion equations and response-basis provenance requirements;
- the PTC fixed-state derivative and source-domain versus CAL-grid entry distinction; and

It does not supply one admitted concrete source/beam perturbation operator that maps a named physical source through source-to-detector/beam and coordinate/scan roles onto the CAL grid. The PTC profile expressly authorizes propagation of an existing companion and creates no response object. CAL also separates its originating Beammap template from later realized response and does not manufacture the missing chain.

Required closure authority: an immutable owner-approved named source/beam response artifact with exact source class and normalization, source-to-detector relation, beam convention, coordinate/scan domain, CAL-grid codomain, support, null space, uncertainty/status, and compatibility with the admitted ALIGN/RTC/CAL parents.

### Independent finding 3 — conditional covariance has no admitted seed

Disposition: **blocking for `TS-U`**.

The package formulas consistently preserve covariance and require axes, units, support, off-diagonal correlations, approximations, and omissions. However, CAL explicitly does not create the statistical measurement covariance, the native interface supplies only uncertainty state/availability, and the packet contains no exact covariance-producing authority for the ordinary CAL-to-PTC route. PTC's conditional formula `C_Z|Theta = J_Theta C_Y J_Theta^T` is therefore a propagation rule, not a source-closed covariance-lineage product. Nuisance, selection, and cross-covariance mechanisms are also explicitly unavailable where not supplied.

Required closure authority: one named, immutable covariance or conditional-uncertainty producer contract on the exact CAL parent domain, with axes, units, normalization, support, correlation scope, approximations, omissions, lifecycle, and cross-package binding. A total-uncertainty claim would additionally require the applicable nuisance and cross-covariance terms.

### Independent finding 4 — no stronger named claim is authorized

Disposition: **blocking for `TS-T`; expected scope boundary rather than a contradiction**.

No admitted authority names a stronger scientific claim and closes all of its response, statistical uncertainty, calibration/atmosphere nuisance, selection uncertainty, cross-covariance, applicability, and owner-decision requirements. The documents explicitly withhold observational qualification, achieved calibration/transfer performance, mapping, significance, beam recovery, and downstream acceptance.

Required closure authority: first name the exact stronger claim and its owner; then bind the complete response and uncertainty authorities for the same identity and support. No lower-tier readiness result may be promoted into that claim.

### Independent finding 5 — runtime realization is intentionally separate

Disposition: **non-blocking for this type-level audit; observation-instance axis unassessed**.

The static authority graph deliberately references runtime Tune, telescope, pointing, geometry/APT, association, WVR, CAL-factor, and configured-plan instances rather than embedding them in the frozen packet. This is coherent because every missing or conflicting required instance has a typed fail-closed result. It means the readiness results above establish no claim that any real observation can presently realize the witness.
