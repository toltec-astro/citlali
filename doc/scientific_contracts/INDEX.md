[Scientific Contract Library Program](README.md). Every new package begins by
linking to that charter and completing the prior-work recovery procedure.
The [prior-work discovery registry](PRIOR_WORK_REGISTRY.md) seeds that search
without replacing package-specific review.

canonical branch, package provenance, retained revision history, and known
recovery gaps before the next program-wide audit.

preserves the sequence from MAP closure through JINC, NOI, filtering,
source/mode work, and FRUIT. The
[ALIGN-to-MAP horizontal owner decisions](ALIGN_TO_MAP_HORIZONTAL_OWNER_DECISIONS_2026-08-26.md)

coordinate/projection boundaries. The later MAP r0.7 targeted closure selects
the initial one-hot projection, types contribution gates, places unique
originals by their own layered AST ALIGN-grid coordinates in the target MAP
WCS, separates fixed-state, PTC full-procedure, and PTC+MAP re-resolved
response, and preserves exact source authority.
