\section{Numerical construction}

\subsection{Occurrence index and broadcast}

Let $p$ identify one exact realized PTC transformed-product/application
generation for one observation $o$. Let $g$ identify a new immutable
coefficient generation bound to $p$, its immutable CAL parent, and the
requested, effective-policy, observation-resolved, and realized family
selection states. The logical coefficient index is the exact detector-time
occurrence $i$. A compact representation may store one scalar only if it binds
the lossless relation
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal B_g(1)=\{(\boldsymbol\iota_i,1):i\in\mathcal D_{g,o}\}.
 \label{eq:broadcast}
\end{equation}
Time, row, column, shape, cardinality, detector label, coordinate equality, or
container position cannot establish broadcast membership.

\subsection{Support and population}

The proposed coefficient support and normalization population are identical:
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal D_{g,o}=\mathcal S^{\mathrm{coef}}_{g,o}
 =\mathcal S^{\mathrm{PTC,out}}_{p,o}.
 \label{eq:domain}
\end{equation}
The family covers every and only exact occurrence in the PTC output support of
its bound generation for one observation. The population spans all detectors,
times, groups, segments, arrays, and networks represented in that support;
none defines a separate normalization population. Observations and product
generations are never pooled.

Equation~\ref{eq:domain} does not make sample validity a support predicate. An
occurrence may carry coefficient one while its signal, coordinate, retention,
or consumer admission is independently unusable. An occurrence outside the
support has no coefficient under this family; absence is not zero.

\subsection{Fixed constant and normalization}

For finite nonempty $\mathcal D_{g,o}$, define
\begin{align}
 c_\star &\equiv 1,\qquad [c_\star]=1,\label{eq:constant}\\
 \widetilde\omega_i&=c_\star,\qquad
 i\in\mathcal D_{g,o},\label{eq:raw}\\
 m_g&=\frac{1}{|\mathcal D_{g,o}|}
       \sum_{j\in\mathcal D_{g,o}}\widetilde\omega_j,\label{eq:mean}\\
 \omega_i&=\mathcal N_{\rm mean1}
   (\widetilde\omega_i;\mathcal D_{g,o})
   \equiv\frac{\widetilde\omega_i}{m_g}=1.
 \label{eq:normalized}
\end{align}
The unit $1$ is dimensionless. Cardinality and summation operate on exact
logical identities; a duplicate identity is a structural conflict rather
than another population member.

The family has no configurable numerical parameter. A generic positive raw
constant $c$ would give
\[
 \frac{c}{|\mathcal D|^{-1}\sum_{j\in\mathcal D}c}=1,
\]
so its scale would be non-identifiable. The fixed value $c_\star=1$ is
therefore part of the family definition. A request carrying any override,
including another positive constant, is not an instance of this family and
cannot be silently ignored, clipped, or normalized into acceptance.

The explicit normalization declares a stable comparison convention. Changing
the operator or population changes scientific identity even though this
constant would remain numerically one. A singleton population is sufficient.
An empty population has no mean and creates no generation or payload; it is
not repaired by an empty pass, zero, or a scalar with inferred empty broadcast.

\subsection{Consumer consequences}

At the proposed JINC boundary,
\begin{equation}
 w_{ip}=\kappa_{ip}\omega_i=\kappa_{ip}.
 \label{eq:jinc}
\end{equation}
The equality concerns only the PTC factor and transfers no JINC ownership.

For the already selected NOI design on an exact MAP-admitted contribution
population,
\begin{equation}
 a_{pi}=G_{pi}\gamma_i=G_{pi},\qquad
 B_d=\sum_p\sum_{\substack{i\in C_p\\
             \operatorname{detector}(i)=d}}a_{pi}.
 \label{eq:noi}
\end{equation}
This does not define $G_{pi}$, change MAP arithmetic, or change the NOI design
law.
