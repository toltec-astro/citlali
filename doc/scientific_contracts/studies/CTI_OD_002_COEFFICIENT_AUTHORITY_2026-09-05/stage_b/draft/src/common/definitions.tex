\section{Physical meaning and intended use}

The family assigns equal PTC coefficient influence to every occurrence
in one explicitly declared coefficient support. It is a transparent baseline:
relative differences in downstream contribution cannot originate from this
PTC coefficient. Other producer and consumer factors can still make total
contributions unequal. MAP retains its own admission, placement, boundary,
exposure, and contribution rules. JINC retains its signed spatial factor
$\kappa_{ip}$ and its own normalization, conditioning, and support.

Uniformity is a statement only about the PTC-owned positive coefficient. It
does not assert equal signal quality, equal exposure, equal response, equal
uncertainty, detector independence, inverse variance, sensitivity,
significance, precision, or optimality. It does not restore an invalid sample,
create output support, or override any consumer decision.

\section{Proposed complete coefficient-handoff profile}

\subsection{Identity, use, and object}

The proposed profile is \ProfileKey. Its scientific package/producer owner is
SCI-PTC, and its proposed actual-owner field is Grant Wilson acting for
SCI-PTC, pending exact confirmation. Its exact use is:
\begin{quote}
For one requested tuple $(g,o,i,c)$, decide whether a realized PTC coefficient
payload is a faithful instance of \FamilyKey\ and is authorized for handoff to
the explicitly named consumer
$c\in\{\mathrm{SCI\mbox{-}MAP},\mathrm{SCI\mbox{-}JINC}\}$.
\end{quote}
The object is one coefficient occurrence plus its generation-level scalar and
broadcast binding. The profile binds the admitted PTC common named-use
fragment and the exact eventual owner-approved source bytes of this contract.
It is not the unsupported generic name
\texttt{SCI-PTC:coefficient\_qc\_population@1}; it is a complete narrower
proposition. While its source or Registry record remains draft or absent,
binding integrity is not authoritative and no eligible evaluation may be
claimed.

\subsection{Applicability and structural gate}

The profile is requested only when the exact family is the resolved selection
for a named consumer and a coefficient handoff is requested. It is applicable
when the exact PTC product exists, $\mathcal D_{g,o}$ is authoritatively
nonempty, and $i\in\mathcal D_{g,o}$. It is inapplicable when authoritative
identity establishes $i\notin\mathcal D_{g,o}$. It has
\texttt{applicability\_unknown} when product, observation, support, target
occurrence membership, or generation binding is missing or conflicting.

The nonexceptionable structural gate requires an active exact Registry record;
actual owner and source binding; complete occurrence, observation, product,
parent, family, generation, selection-lineage, support, payload-publication,
target-broadcast, profile, and named-consumer identities; and consistent scopes
and immutable provenance. The target population must contain exact unique
occurrence identities, and the target broadcast must bind the identified
publication slot to that population and generation. An expected publication
slot may be identified while its bytes remain absent or unavailable; that
availability is a later payload-fidelity input. Missing or conflicting target
broadcast binding, duplicate or ambiguous occurrence identity, inferred
ordering, cross-observation pooling, or cross-generation pairing fails the
gate and makes the decision unavailable rather than use-specifically
ineligible.

Structural establishment fixes what population and publication are being
asked about; it does not assert that the stored payload faithfully realizes
them. Only after that question exists does UQ-05 compare the bound bytes and a
complete stored payload-membership manifest with the authoritative target.
Thus a duplicate identity in the authoritative population is structural,
whereas a repeated stored payload member relative to an already unique target
population is a payload-fidelity violation.

\subsection{Exact profile inputs and truth-domain rules}

The profile consumes only the following owner-bound inputs after the structural
gate establishes their identities: the resolved family-selection record and
its parameter field; the PTC compatibility permission for the already-bound
product and coefficient generations; the immutable PTC output-support record
and exact member identities; the family-definition record naming constant,
unit, operator, and population; the typed payload-availability record,
published scalar bytes, declared decoder, decoded unit, complete stored
payload-membership manifest, publication digest, and authoritative integrity
result; the exact named-consumer permission; and immutable lifecycle links and
overwrite/nonmutation attestations. Sample validity,
retention, consumer admission, response, covariance, and diagnostics are
preserved inputs for provenance but are not permission predicates.

For each restriction below, $\mathbf T$ means its complete authoritative input
set positively satisfies the stated predicate; $\mathbf F$ means the complete
authoritative input set explicitly violates it; $\mathbf U$ means a required
input or complete assertion is missing or the check was not performed; and
$\mathbf C$ means authoritative predicate inputs for the same structurally
established object are contradictory or ambiguous. A parent, occurrence,
source, or generation identity mismatch is handled earlier by the structural
gate and is therefore decision unavailable. It is not converted to
$\mathbf F$ here.

The payload-fidelity input in UQ-05 is the producer QC primitive, not an
assertion of this profile's outcome. It is evaluated only after the exact
unique $\mathcal D_{g,o}$, payload-publication identity, and target broadcast
binding are structurally established. Its disjoint truth-domain rule is exact:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\mathbf T$ only when SCI-PTC authoritatively reports
\texttt{computed\_published}, the bound bytes are readable under the declared
decoder, the decoded scalar and unit are exactly $(1,1)$, the publication
digest matches, and a complete authoritative stored membership manifest
contains every and only each target identity exactly once;
\item $\mathbf F$ only when a complete authoritative fidelity check establishes
a decoded non-one value or wrong unit; a digest or other integrity failure;
corruption, truncation, decoder-invalid bytes, or known unrepresentability,
including a corrupt scalar that cannot be read; or a complete stored membership
manifest that omits, adds, or repeats any member relative to the established
target population;
\item $\mathbf U$ when the payload, bytes, decoder, digest, integrity result,
complete check, or complete stored membership manifest is absent or
unavailable, or when the payload is unreadable without an authoritative
fidelity cause or verdict; and
\item $\mathbf C$ when authoritative fidelity records for the same bound
payload contradict one another or are ambiguous. The conflict is preserved and
composes as unresolved $\mathbf U$ unless another required predicate is
decisively $\mathbf F$.
\end{itemize}
Missing or conflicting structural identity never enters this UQ-05 partition;
it stops the question at the structural gate.

\section{Proposed boundary/profile successors}

\subsection{MAP}

The proposed boundary identity is \MapBoundaryDraft. It changes only the
coefficient facet needed for this family: a selected exact \FamilyKey\ payload
may occupy the existing coefficient slot only after an approved family record
explicitly grants SCI-MAP permission and the exact \ProfileKey\ decision is
requested, applicable, eligible, and realized. The payload is dimensionless
$\gamma_i=\omega_i=1$ on the matching occurrence. Every frozen MAP identity,
signal, retention, coordinate, response, uncertainty, exposure, influence,
admission, placement, membership, contribution, failure, and classification
rule remains unchanged.

MAP must verify exact family, version, generation, parent, occurrence, support,
selection, profile, and permission binding, retrieve the authorized payload,
and classify it independently. Only a finite strictly positive value may enter
its existing arithmetic. The family does not make an occurrence
MAP-admissible and defines no MAP-local factor. Until the family, profile,
boundary, and exact bindings are adopted and active, the route is unavailable.

\subsection{JINC}

The proposed boundary identity is \JincBoundaryDraft. It changes only the
JINC-facing PTC coefficient facet: the same selected \FamilyKey\ payload may
enter only after an approved family record separately grants SCI-JINC
permission and the exact \ProfileKey\ decision is requested, applicable,
eligible, and realized. JINC independently classifies $\omega_i=1$ and, after
all existing JINC gates, applies Equation~\ref{eq:jinc}. This creates no
PTC ownership of $\kappa_{ip}$, signed normalization, conditioning, support,
or bundle semantics and no base-v0.1 JINC response, covariance, or uncertainty
product. All other frozen JINC constraints remain unchanged. Until separate
JINC permission and every exact binding is active, the route is unavailable.

\subsection{No inference or fallback}

MAP permission never implies JINC permission and JINC permission never implies
MAP permission. Neither consumer may infer this family from its unit, the
numerical value one, another consumer's availability, a label, or a missing
selection. Absent selection without an authorized mode default, unregistered
family, incompatible generation, missing payload/QC, or missing permission
makes the route unavailable. There is no unity, alternate-family,
neighboring-product, direct-CAL, no-op-PTC, reconstruction, or
generation-repair fallback. PTC-disabled operation remains the RTC-terminal
export route and creates no PTC, MAP, or JINC product.
