\section{Normative proposed requirements}

Every requirement below is a proposal pending scientific-owner adoption.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.15\linewidth}p{0.76\linewidth}}
\toprule
ID & Requirement\\
\midrule
\endhead
UFC-REQ-001 & The family shall have exact identity \FamilyKey, scientific
owner SCI-PTC, and no active or registered status until owner approval and
exact Registry binding.\\
UFC-REQ-002 & The logical index shall be exact detector-time occurrence $i$;
any scalar publication shall structurally bind its identity to the complete
lossless target relation in Equation~\ref{eq:broadcast}. Its stored membership
manifest shall be separately evaluated as payload-fidelity evidence.\\
UFC-REQ-003 & For one observation and bound PTC product generation,
coefficient support and normalization population shall both equal the exact
PTC output support as in Equation~\ref{eq:domain}.\\
UFC-REQ-004 & On a finite nonempty population, the raw constant shall be exact
dimensionless one and normalization shall be the arithmetic mean-one operator
in Equations~\ref{eq:constant}--\ref{eq:normalized}.\\
UFC-REQ-005 & The family shall accept no numerical parameter. Any constant,
unit, operator, or population override shall require another registered family
or version and shall not be silently ignored.\\
UFC-REQ-006 & A singleton population shall be valid. An empty population, or
an authoritative population with duplicate, ambiguous, or conflicting
identity, shall create no realized coefficient generation and shall not be
repaired by zero, unity, deduplication, or an empty pass. Stored payload
membership is assessed only after a unique target population exists.\\
UFC-REQ-007 & Occurrences outside coefficient support shall have no coefficient
under this family; absence shall remain distinct from numerical zero.\\
UFC-REQ-008 & Every generation shall bind exact transformed product,
application generation, CAL parent, observation, selection lineage, support,
family, profile, and publication identities without mutating any parent or
earlier generation.\\
UFC-REQ-009 & Repeated realizations shall reproduce exact values and broadcast
membership but retain distinct immutable generation identities; numerical
equality alone shall not permit borrowing.\\
UFC-REQ-010 & The family record shall state SCI-MAP and SCI-JINC permissions
separately. Each proposed permission requires independent owner adoption and
neither shall imply the other.\\
UFC-REQ-011 & Coefficient fidelity shall be evaluated only under the complete
proposed \ProfileKey\ record; the unsupported generic coefficient-QC
placeholder shall not be used or simulated.\\
UFC-REQ-012 & The profile shall bind exact use, owner, source, object/domain,
restrictions, no-exception rule, response/uncertainty roles, compatibility,
supersession, missing/conflict behavior, aggregation status, and lifecycle.\\
UFC-REQ-013 & Structural target identity, stored payload membership, payload
availability, producer fidelity, sample validity, MAP admission, JINC
admission, and each consumer's numerical classification and contribution shall
remain distinct.\\
UFC-REQ-014 & Every applicable fact and predicate shall use T/F/U/C semantics;
silence shall be unknown, conflict shall be preserved, known exclusion shall
dominate unrelated unknowns, and no evidence shall be erased.\\
UFC-REQ-015 & Requested, applicability, eligibility, and realization shall be
four independent axes, including uninstantiated eligibility where no question
is evaluated. A completed false predicate shall yield a realized ineligible
profile evaluation, not a failed profile artifact.\\
UFC-REQ-016 & All profile restrictions and structural invariants shall be
nonexceptionable. No exception from VAL, another use, consumer, or mode shall
alter them.\\
UFC-REQ-017 & Structural missing/conflict/ambiguity shall stop the question;
within an established target, absent evidence or unexplained unreadability
shall be $\mathbf U$, while authoritatively established corruption or
missing/extra/repeated stored membership shall be $\mathbf F$. These states,
conflict, exact zero, non-finite, and unrepresentable cases shall remain
distinct and follow the stated failure scopes.\\
UFC-REQ-018 & Response and uncertainty availability shall be advisory for this
coefficient-fidelity profile; exact upstream states shall be preserved and
neither identity response nor zero uncertainty shall be inferred.\\
UFC-REQ-019 & The coefficient shall be nonprecision and shall not be
interpreted as inverse variance, sensitivity, significance, fitted loading,
scatter, validity, exposure, response, covariance, independence, or
optimality.\\
UFC-REQ-020 & The MAP successor shall change only its coefficient binding,
retain all MAP-owned arithmetic and gates, and require MAP's independent
finite-strictly-positive classification before use.\\
UFC-REQ-021 & The JINC successor shall change only its PTC-coefficient binding,
retain JINC ownership of $\kappa_{ip}$ and its arithmetic/gates, and create no
new JINC response, covariance, uncertainty, or bundle role.\\
UFC-REQ-022 & NOI compatibility shall require the frozen exact lossless
rational-parent record for the exact MAP and coefficient generations. Missing
binding shall leave NOI unavailable and shall not change its design law.\\
UFC-REQ-023 & No family or mode default shall be inferred or proposed. Missing
selection or authority shall make the route unavailable without fallback.\\
UFC-REQ-024 & Every material change of definition, support, operator,
population, unit, profile, permission, boundary, or missing/conflict behavior
shall create a versioned successor and shall not rewrite an earlier
evaluation.\\
UFC-REQ-025 & Algebraic conformance, implementation conformity,
representation/response fidelity, observational performance, and production
readiness shall remain separate evidence claims.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
