\section{Normative proposed engineering requirements}

Every requirement is an open proposal pending scientific-owner adoption.

\begin{longtable}{p{0.15\linewidth}p{0.76\linewidth}}
\toprule
ID & Requirement\\
\midrule
\endhead
UFC-REQ-001 & The family shall retain exact identity \FamilyKey, owner
SCI-PTC, and inactive draft status until exact owner-approved source and
Registry bindings exist.\\
UFC-REQ-002 & The coefficient index shall be exact detector-time occurrence
\(i\), distinct from spatial index \(p\). A scalar shall bind occurrences by
either stored compact reference \(\widehat R_g\) independently matched to
authoritative target \(R_q\), or complete enumerated manifest \(M_g\); order,
shape, label, or cardinality shall not substitute.\\
UFC-REQ-003 & Each coefficient realization shall bind one independently
complete PTC publication generation \(q\), tagged as a segment, scan, or
bounded within-scan/segment chunk in one observation. Its support shall equal
the exact output support of \(q\); completion of unrelated units or the whole
observation shall not gate handoff.\\
UFC-REQ-004 & On finite nonempty support, the raw constant shall be exact
dimensionless one and arithmetic-mean-to-one normalization shall be evaluated
analytically as one. A literal population-wide reduction or stored
per-occurrence array shall not be required.\\
UFC-REQ-005 & The family shall accept no numerical parameter. An altered
constant, unit rule, operator, or population rule shall be another
family/version and shall not be ignored, clipped, or normalized into
acceptance.\\
UFC-REQ-006 & A singleton target shall be valid. Empty support shall create no
instance. Duplicate, ambiguous, or conflicting identity in the authoritative
target shall fail structural establishment and shall not be deduplicated or
repaired.\\
UFC-REQ-007 & An occurrence authoritatively outside \(q\)'s support shall have
no coefficient under this family; absence shall remain distinct from numerical
zero.\\
UFC-REQ-008 & Every \(q\) and \(g\) shall bind exact application generation,
CAL parent, observation, segment/scan/chunk bounds, ordinary-route rank
provenance where applicable, selection lineage, support, source, and durable
required components/links without mutating a parent or earlier realization.\\
UFC-REQ-009 & Immutable family, scalar, unit, integrity, compatibility, and
permission evaluations may be shared once at their exact scope. Every
occurrence-level handoff shall retain a logical association to those results
and its own membership/request fact; redundant per-occurrence materialization
shall not be required.\\
UFC-REQ-010 & SCI-MAP and SCI-JINC permission facts shall be separate. A
denial, absence, unknown, or conflict for one shall not change payload fidelity
or determine the other.\\
UFC-REQ-011 & An explicit request for \ProfileKey shall set the request axis
independently of family selection. After the selection-record identity is
structurally bound, a known different value shall reach UQ-01 and be false,
missing value shall be unknown, and conflicting value claims shall conflict.
No named-profile request shall create no evaluation.\\
UFC-REQ-012 & The complete profile shall bind exact use, owner, source,
\((g,q,i,c)\) object, applicability, shared-object gate, restrictions,
fact-level truth rules, no-exception rule, advisory roles, compatibility,
supersession, scopes, aggregation status, and lifecycle.\\
UFC-REQ-013 & Structural identity, scalar value, unit, integrity, stored
membership, payload availability, producer validity, named permission, sample
validity, consumer admission, numerical classification, and contribution
shall remain distinct facts.\\
UFC-REQ-014 & \(Q_{\rm scalar},Q_{\rm unit},Q_{\rm integrity},Q_{\rm member}\)
shall be independently T/F/U/C. Any independent false shall make UQ-05 false;
otherwise conflict shall remain conflict, otherwise unknown shall remain
unknown, and only all true shall yield true. Contradiction within one fact
shall remain one conflict rather than a selected false.\\
UFC-REQ-015 & Requested, applicability, eligibility, and profile-artifact
realization shall be independent axes. A completed false predicate shall yield
a realized ineligible evaluation; a completed unresolved evaluation may be
realized and decision unavailable; failed realization shall mean the profile
artifact itself failed.\\
UFC-REQ-016 & Every profile restriction and structural invariant shall be
nonexceptionable. No VAL, consumer, mode, or other-use exception shall alter
the result.\\
UFC-REQ-017 & Failure scope shall follow the defective object. A wrong
\(q,g\) pair or other malformed reference in one request shall remain local;
a corrupt/misbound authoritative \(q\)-to-\(g\) or other shared object shall
affect every reference to that object; payload false shall affect that
payload's handoffs; permission state shall affect only its named consumer.
Every cause and independent fact state shall be retained.\\
UFC-REQ-018 & Response and uncertainty availability shall be advisory and
neutral for coefficient fidelity. Exact upstream states shall be preserved,
and neither identity response nor zero uncertainty shall be inferred.\\
UFC-REQ-019 & The coefficient shall be nonprecision and shall not mean inverse
variance, sensitivity, significance, fitted loading, scatter, sample validity,
exposure, response, covariance, independence, optimality, or a policy
default.\\
UFC-REQ-020 & The MAP successor shall change only its coefficient binding,
retain all MAP-owned gates and arithmetic, and require MAP's independent
finite-strictly-positive classification before use.\\
UFC-REQ-021 & The JINC successor shall change only its PTC-coefficient binding,
retain JINC ownership of spatial index \(p\), \(\kappa_{ip}\), sign,
arithmetic, gates, and support, and create no new response, covariance,
uncertainty, or bundle role.\\
UFC-REQ-022 & NOI compatibility shall require the frozen exact lossless
rational-parent record for the exact MAP and coefficient generations. Missing
binding shall leave NOI unavailable and shall not change its design law.\\
UFC-REQ-023 & No family or publication-size default shall be inferred. Missing
selection or authority shall produce no unity or alternate-family fallback.
PTC-disabled shall remain RTC-terminal. On the admitted first ordinary route,
rank zero, noninteger rank, or unrealizable positive rank shall fail closed and
shall not select another route or produce a coefficient. No claim is made
about unapproved identity or zero-rank route families.\\
UFC-REQ-024 & A change to a scientific family/profile/permission/boundary rule
shall create the corresponding versioned successor. New application, product,
support population, payload, request, or evaluation under unchanged rules
shall create a new immutable realization, not a new family version; no earlier
record shall be rewritten.\\
UFC-REQ-025 & Algebraic conformance, implementation conformity,
representation/response fidelity, observational performance, and production
readiness shall remain separate evidence claims. No implementation or
validation result shall be inferred from this specification.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
