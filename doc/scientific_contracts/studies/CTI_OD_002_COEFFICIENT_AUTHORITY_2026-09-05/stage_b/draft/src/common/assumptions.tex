\section{Assumptions, decision semantics, and lifecycle}

\subsection{Scientific assumptions and limits}

This definition assumes only that one exact realized PTC product generation
exists for one observation, its exact output support can be identified, and
the support is finite and nonempty. It assumes no statistical distribution,
stationarity, independence, homoscedasticity, detector equivalence, exposure
equivalence, residual model, sensitivity model, or response model. It neither
estimates nor ranks data quality.

The coefficient value is an exact policy constant and therefore introduces no
sampling uncertainty. That statement is limited to the coefficient itself. It
does not assign zero to calibration, signal, response, covariance,
cross-observation, support-selection, family-selection, representation, or
performance uncertainty. Unknown remains unknown and absent covariance never
means independence.

\subsection{Knowledge and four independent axes}

Every applicable fact and profile predicate uses
$\{\mathbf T,\mathbf F,\mathbf U,\mathbf C\}$ with the admitted VAL meanings.
Silence is $\mathbf U$. A structural contradiction blocks establishment of the
question. A non-gating $\mathbf C$ is preserved and composes as unresolved
$\mathbf U$. One decisive $\mathbf F$ yields ineligible even when another
non-gating predicate is unresolved; without a false, any required unresolved
predicate yields decision unavailable; all required predicates true yields
eligible. Every cause is retained without evidence-erasing short circuit.

The \ProfileKey\ record carries independent axes:
\begin{itemize}
\item $R=$ requested or not requested. A family not selected and handoff not
requested has no eligibility evaluation.
\item $A=$ applicable, inapplicable, or applicability unknown under the
profile definition. Known outside-support occurrences have
$E=\varnothing_E$.
\item $E=$ eligible, ineligible, decision unavailable, or uninstantiated
$\varnothing_E$ where no question is evaluated.
\item $Z=$ realized, incomplete, failed, or not produced for the profile
evaluation. Coefficient payload availability remains a separate typed fact.
\end{itemize}
A realized decision-unavailable record is a successful account of missing
authority. If the evaluation artifact itself is incomplete, failed, or not
produced, it cannot manufacture an eligibility assertion.

\subsection{Payload order and failure scope}

Processing first establishes identity, parentage, profile authority,
applicability, and producer validity; then excludes producer-invalid payloads;
then checks an admitted required payload against exact unity. Finiteness or a
numerical value of one cannot supply missing authority, validity, support, or
permission. MAP and JINC subsequently perform their own positive/zero/
negative/non-finite/unrepresentable classification.

\subsection{Immutable lifecycle and repetition}

Creating $g$ never changes transformed product $p$ or an earlier coefficient
generation. A change in support, product/application generation, observation,
family/version, operator, population, unit, profile, permission, or selection
lineage creates a new scientific identity and preserves all earlier parents.

Repeated realization from the same exact inputs must yield the same
mathematical payload and membership relation, but each realization retains its
own immutable generation identity. Equal values do not authorize substitution
or borrowing. Cross-generation use requires an explicit compatibility record
binding both generations and preserving their claims. This proposal provides
no general compatibility alias.

\section{Decision boundary}

The draft has no blocked mathematical definition. Every consequential new
choice remains an open author proposal in the owner-decision ledger. Until
those choices are adopted and exact successor source/Registry bindings exist,
the family, profile, permissions, and boundary successors remain unavailable
for evaluation or use. Approval of this family would not close deferred
families, mode defaults, or independently governed route prerequisites.

\texttt{OWNER\_DECISION\_LEDGER.md}; the ledger carries the full owner,
evidence, resolution-authority, date, and affected-document fields.
