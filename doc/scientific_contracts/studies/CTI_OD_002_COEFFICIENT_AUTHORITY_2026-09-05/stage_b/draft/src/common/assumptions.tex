\section{Scientific assumptions, uncertainty, and route limits}

The family assumes only a completed, exact PTC publication generation \(q\)
with identifiable finite nonempty output support. It assumes no statistical
distribution, stationarity, independence, equal detector response, equal
exposure, residual model, sensitivity model, or covariance model. The
coefficient is defined rather than estimated and ranks no data quality.

The exact policy constant introduces no coefficient sampling uncertainty.
This does not assign zero to signal, calibration, response, covariance,
support-selection, family-selection, representation, or performance
uncertainty. Unknown remains unknown, and absent covariance does not mean
independence.

\subsection{Ordinary-route and rank provenance}

Frozen SCI-PTC v0.1/r0.5 requirement 090 defines the admitted first ordinary
PTC route: the parent \(q\) is an explicitly requested calibrated-\(x\),
configured-rank, group-local PCA/SVD operation with exact CAL parent, segment,
output identity, lifecycle, and failure state. Requirement 094 requires an
integer rank at least one for each group, or one explicitly common positive
rank applied to named groups; every group verifies realizability on its own
support and degeneracy state. Requirement 095 says rank zero, noninteger rank,
or an unrealizable positive rank fails closed with exact group and cause. It
does not become centering-only output, get clipped or substituted, become
PTC-disabled, select RTC-terminal export, or produce a map.

Consequently, this coefficient can be instantiated on the ordinary route only
from a completed \(q\) produced after those rank obligations pass. The
PTC-disabled route remains separately RTC-terminal and produces no PTC
coefficient. These inherited statements provide no authority for an identity
PTC route, a zero-rank route family, or any other route.

\subsection{Family rules, realizations, and immutable change}

A family-rule version defines the constant, unit, normalization operator,
support rule, representation permissions, and profile semantics. Changing one
of those scientific rules creates the appropriate versioned family, profile,
permission, or boundary successor.

Applying unchanged rules to a different application generation, observation,
publication unit, or exact support population creates new immutable \(q\) and
\(g\) realizations under the same family version. It does not version the
family. A published realization is never rewritten: a corrected or changed
actual support produces a new realization and preserves the earlier record.
Numerical equality alone does not authorize cross-generation borrowing.

\subsection{Knowledge states and four independent axes}

Every fact uses \(\{\mathbf T,\mathbf F,\mathbf U,\mathbf C\}\). Silence is
unknown. Contradictory authoritative claims about the same fact remain
\(\mathbf C\). Across independent facts, a decisive \(\mathbf F\) dominates
unrelated unknown or conflict for eligibility while every cause remains
recorded. With no false, any required unknown or conflict yields decision
unavailable; all required facts true yields eligible.

The named profile carries independent axes:

\begin{itemize}
\item \(R\): requested or not requested, set by the explicit named-profile
request rather than by the selected-family value;
\item \(A\): applicable, inapplicable, or applicability unknown;
\item \(E\): eligible, ineligible, decision unavailable, or uninstantiated
where no question is evaluated; and
\item \(Z\): realized, incomplete, failed, or not produced for the profile
evaluation artifact, separate from payload availability.
\end{itemize}

A completed false predicate produces a successfully realized negative
evaluation, \(Z=\) realized and \(E=\) ineligible. A completed record of
missing authority may likewise have \(Z=\) realized and \(E=\) decision
unavailable. \(Z=\) failed is reserved for failure to complete or publish the
profile-evaluation artifact.

\subsection{Noncircular UQ-07 construction and finalization}

UQ-07 is staged and never requires an already completed evaluation of itself.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Construction allocates the evaluation identity and snapshots the
pre-existing request, Registry, parent, lineage, support, payload, permission,
and UQ-01--UQ-06 fact references.
\item Before sealing, UQ-07 evaluates only those pre-existing lineage,
nonmutation, scope, and write-once-target facts. A known violation is
\(\mathbf F\); missing/conflicting evidence is \(\mathbf U/\mathbf C\).
\item Finalization durably writes the complete result and its retained causes,
then the lifecycle envelope records \(Z=\) realized. If durable finalization
fails, the envelope records failed/incomplete as available; no completed
UQ-07 value or eligibility assertion is invented.
\end{enumerate}

Later discovery never rewrites the sealed record. It produces a successor
evaluation or realization with explicit supersession and preserved cause.
