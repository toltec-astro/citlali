\section{Engineering interpretation of the shared core}

An eventual implementation assessment should treat UFC-REQ-001--010 as the
family payload and lifecycle surface, UFC-REQ-011--018 as the PTC-owned
profile/evaluation surface, UFC-REQ-019--024 as consumer, NOI, fallback, and
change-control boundaries, and UFC-REQ-025 as the evidence ceiling. This
grouping changes no requirement meaning. The authoritative mapping for review
is the companion \texttt{CROSSWALK.md}; exact draft identity and provenance
bindings are in \texttt{PROPOSED\_REGISTRY\_AND\_BOUNDARY\_RECORDS.md} and the
artifact manifest.

Conformance evidence must report exact source, family, profile, product,
coefficient-generation, observation, occurrence-support, consumer, and
evaluation identities. A numerical array of ones by itself establishes none
of those bindings.
