# PTC coefficient successor — uniform-first Scope Brief

## Program adherence and prior-work recovery

This scope follows the [charter and approved-roadmap binding](references/PROCESS_AND_SCOPE.md)
and the [completed recovery synthesis](PRIOR_WORK.md). It adopts the scoped
[frozen PTC predecessor](references/PTC_COEFFICIENTS.md),
[approved registry framework](references/REGISTRY_FRAMEWORK.md),
[MAP coefficient boundary](references/MAP_BOUNDARY.md),
[JINC coefficient boundary](references/JINC_BOUNDARY.md),
[NOI derived-parent limits](references/NOI_BINDING.md), and
[VAL named-use rules](references/VAL_RULES.md). These seven references are the
entire scientific/process reference set, with exact bytes in `MANIFEST.json`.

Earlier definitions and ownership decisions are adopted, existing generic
construction is cited rather than re-derived, and later sensitivity,
residual-scatter and hybrid families are deferred. Historical implementation,
audit, repair and validation material is excluded. New work is one complete
uniform/constant family and the common registry-entry/QC contract needed to
describe it. The recovery opening and exact inputs require release approval
before authorship; preparation of this first scope is already approved.

## Scientific question and boundary

Define a PTC-owned positive analysis/gridding coefficient family with uniform
or constant meaning on its explicitly declared domain. Specify what uniformity
means across detectors, time, groups, segments and normalization populations,
and propose a complete reproducible numerical definition. A family name alone
is insufficient. The numerical value or parameterization, units, normalization
operator/domain, scientific support and QC details are not selected in this
brief. Derive and recommend them for owner review.

The same PTC-owned registry contains explicit permission for MAP, JINC or
both. This first family must be considered for both consumers, with separate
explicit compatibility and permission conclusions; do not infer one from the
other. PTC owns the produced coefficient values and their immutable
generation. MAP and JINC consume those values and own their distinct
admission and numerical classifications. JINC alone supplies its signed
spatial factor. No MAP/JINC estimator, support policy, response role or
product bundle is redesigned.

There is no new request default or mode recommendation. Uniformity does not
mean a hidden unity fallback, a valid zero where data are missing, restored
sample validity, independence, inverse variance, precision or significance.
Preserve the PTC-enabled transformed-product and exact-occurrence requirements;
a new coefficient entry does not create a PTC-disabled map route.

## Required scientific outputs

1. **Common entry contract.** State exact family/version and PTC owner;
   detector or detector-time index and broadcast relation; factors and their
   domains; unit; normalization operator and population; support; immutable
   parent and transformed-product compatibility; uncertainty meaning;
   lifecycle; named consumer permissions; prohibited interpretations; and
   missing, invalid, conflicting and unrepresentable states.
2. **One proposed uniform/constant definition.** Distinguish a fixed numeric
   constant from any allowed parameter, and define where it is constant and
   how normalization changes its value or meaning. Explain the purpose and
   limits of the family without claiming optimality. Address empty or
   insufficient domains, inadmissible parameters, changed support, repeated
   realization, index/broadcast ambiguity and prohibited cross-generation
   borrowing. Preserve every existing transformed parent when a new
   coefficient generation is created.
3. **Complete PTC coefficient-use QC.** Declare exact scientific use, inputs,
   applicability, decision-relevant restrictions, exception permissions,
   T/F/U/C interpretation, missing/conflict behavior, failure scope and four
   independent decision axes. Distinguish structural identity binding,
   sample validity, payload availability, producer QC, consumer admission and
   numerical contribution. Do not turn unrelated metadata or advisory
   diagnostics into gates. Do not use the unsupported generic QC placeholder
   as a policy or create an empty pass to simulate missing authority.
4. **Proposed boundary/profile successors.** State the changes needed at the
   PTC-to-MAP and PTC-to-JINC boundaries and in exact VAL source/profile
   bindings, retaining their existing consumer arithmetic and unrelated
   scientific meanings. Identify compatible and incompatible sources and
   generations, no-inference/no-fallback behavior, and the scientific owner
   responsible for each restriction. Draft records do not become active
   Registry entries through authorship.
5. **NOI compatibility limit.** Preserve its already selected MAP-derived
   balance and lossless rational-parent binding. Assess the proposed family's
   parent identity/representation obligations without changing NOI's design
   law or MAP arithmetic. State conditional availability where exact parent
   bindings are missing. Do not register a separate PTC NOI-balance family,
   admit a JINC-hosted NOI route, or approve NOI's four pending profile
   successors.
6. **Traceability and scientific review.** Provide common definitions,
   equations, assumptions, requirements, predictions and edge-case classes;
   two conformant views; a requirement/prediction crosswalk; a scientific
   validity/uncertainty table; and a ledger separating recovered approved
   facts, author proposals, unresolved questions and deferred work. Give
   precise evidence requirements for eventual conformance and scientific
   validation without claiming they have been performed.

## Deferred and excluded scope

APT-sensitivity, residual-scatter, hybrid and learned coefficient families
are deferred. No sensitivity estimator, residual statistic, adaptive learning,
default, mode policy or optimization is selected. Keep PTC fitting/rank,
centering, calibration, AST/ALIGN, MAP-local policies, JINC kernel design,
NOI target/design law, covariance construction, filtering, FRUIT, Pointing
and OOF outside this derivation. Their established inputs remain constraints;
unresolved prerequisites stay unavailable rather than being supplied here.

## Acceptance boundary

The first deliverable is a scientific draft for owner review, not a frozen
successor or usable numerical route. Any material contradiction with an
admitted source is returned as a precise question rather than resolved by
reopening another package. Every consequential author choice remains visibly
proposed until the scientific owner adopts it. Closing the wider coefficient
authority decision requires exact dispositions for each covered route,
including explicit unavailable states where appropriate; one family cannot
silently close all remaining candidates or routes.
