## Recovered scientific content

PTC's frozen generic construction, `tilde-gamma = f_gamma(eta; chi)` followed
by a declared normalization operator/domain, specifies a family interface,
not a registered numerical formula (P03, coefficient section). REQ-054
requires the statistic/factors, index family, units, normalization, support,
lifecycle, permissions and prohibited meanings; REQ-055 forbids mutating an
earlier transformed product when coefficients are re-estimated (P02).

O01 fixes registry architecture and three discovery seeds; J02--J04 extend
explicit consumer permission to JINC. These close the framework question
without supplying a concrete entry. The PTC/MAP frozen open ledgers and the
later approved framework therefore describe different temporal states, not
an instruction to ask the owner to choose a universal formula again.

For the selected ordinary NOI route, the previously defined detector balance
mass is the sum of exact positive MAP contribution coefficients for that
detector over the frozen admitted population: `a_pi = G_pi gamma_i > 0`.
The earlier owner/boundary notation `B_d` and final r0.5 notation `beta_d`
refer to that design role, not another PTC coefficient family. The final r0.5
parent-binding record requires a lossless canonical rational lift of those
exact MAP parent values, with family/generation/payload and representation
source/digest bound (N02--N03). It governs NOI design only, not MAP arithmetic.
Recomputing from rounded display text or another generation is forbidden.
The four r0.5 profile successors remain proposed, unapproved, Registry-unbound
and unevaluable under N01/N04. Completing PTC coefficients cannot approve them.
