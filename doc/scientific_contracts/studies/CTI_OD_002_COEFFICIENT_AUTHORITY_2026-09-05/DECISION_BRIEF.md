# CTI-OD-002 — proposed first coefficient-contract scope

Status: manager recommendation for owner disposition, 2026-09-05.
This is not a scientific selection, registration, default or launch approval.
Exact source identities and recovery dispositions are in
[PRIOR_WORK.md](PRIOR_WORK.md) and [SOURCE_MANIFEST.json](SOURCE_MANIFEST.json).

## Recommendation

Authorize preparation of a bounded PTC successor author packet covering the
common registry-entry contract and **one uniform/constant coefficient family**,
with explicit proposed permission for MAP and JINC. Keep APT-sensitivity and
residual-scatter families as separately tracked next entries. Select no mode
default. This stages the work around a complete reference entry while the
other candidates' numerical prerequisites are resolved; it makes no claim
that uniform coefficients are scientifically preferable for an observation.

Approval of this recommendation would authorize scope/packet preparation,
not adoption of a constant value or normalization, dispatch of an author,
Registry admission, implementation, or closure of CTI-OD-002. The complete
sanitized author input remains subject to the charter's exact scope approval.

## Decisions already made

- PTC owns **one versioned registry**, with explicit MAP, JINC or joint
  permission per family/version. Neither consumer's permission implies the
  other's. A single mandatory universal weighting formula is not the task.
- A user selects an allowed family; a default requires a separate explicit,
  versioned mode policy. Requested, effective, observation-resolved and
  realized selections remain distinct. Missing selection, registration,
  compatible payload or required QC gives an unavailable route, with no
  hidden unity or alternate-family fallback.
- PTC coefficients are scalar analysis/gridding quantities. Precision,
  inverse variance and significance require separate established authority.
  MAP and JINC consume the PTC values without reconstructing the formula;
  JINC separately owns its signed spatial factor.
- NOI's design balance is already derived from exact frozen MAP-admitted
  contribution coefficients. It is not a third independently selected PTC
  weighting family. Its frozen r0.5 exact rational-parent requirement and
  currently unapproved profile successors remain binding limitations.

These are recovered decisions. Their incorporation into frozen PTC/MAP
successors is still outstanding; this brief does not rewrite the freezes.

## Required output of the proposed successor work

1. A shared scientific definition and two conformant human-facing views for
   the common entry contract and the approved first family, preserving frozen
   PTC meaning outside the bounded change. Include exact family/version,
   permitted consumers, factors/domains/units, normalization/support,
   parentage, generation, uncertainty meaning and prohibited interpretations.
2. A PTC-owned complete named-use coefficient/QC proposition, with exact
   inputs, restrictions, T/F/U/C handling, exceptions, missing/conflict
   behavior and requested/applicable/eligible/realized state. Separate
   metadata binding, sample validity, coefficient availability, producer QC,
   consumer admission and numerical contribution. Informational diagnostics
   remain informational unless specifically granted decision authority.
3. Proposed exact boundary and VAL source/profile successors, with explicit
   compatibility and unchanged consumer arithmetic. The unsupported generic
   `SCI-PTC:coefficient_qc_population@1` cannot be activated by name, alias or
   a vacuous pass. Actual profile approval and Registry admission are later
   exact owner dispositions, not effects of authoring.
4. A crosswalk and owner-decision record that identify what is resolved and
   unavailable. Preserve the NOI MAP-derived balance and lossless rational
   parent binding without redefining its method or approving its four r0.5
   profile successors. Preserve all unrelated MAP-local successor decisions.

## What completion would and would not establish

A reviewed and owner-approved exact family, complete boundary/profile
successors and immutable source bindings could supply coefficient authority
for explicitly permitted routes. It would not establish application
conformity, enable production, close unrelated MAP or NOI gates, or select an
ordinary-science, Pointing or OOF default.

CTI-OD-002 can close only after every covered route has an exact owner-bound
coefficient/QC/profile disposition or an explicit unavailable disposition.
One completed family does not silently resolve the remaining candidates or
routes. CTI-OD-001 remains accepted/closed; CTI-OD-003--006 remain open and
CTI-OD-007 remains inherited/closed.
