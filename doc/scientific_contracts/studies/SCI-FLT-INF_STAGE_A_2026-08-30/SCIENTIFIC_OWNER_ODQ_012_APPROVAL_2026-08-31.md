## Approved minimal-derived-product boundary

Base v0.1 permits only minimal immutable, non-mutating FLT/NOI companion
products required by its selected signal, uncertainty, lifecycle, and
cross-package boundaries. An NOI-derived scalar shall not modify `A_hat`,
`D`, `Q`, template scaling, a parent coefficient, or an already realized
filtered product. Any future empirical coefficient is a new immutable
successor with its own estimand, region, estimator, dependence, uncertainty,
consumer permission, and unavailable state. It is not variance, inverse
variance, precision, calibration, or estimator normalization by name or
shape.

Any future conditional-scale standardized amplitude product is also a
separately identified derived method over an exact immutable numerator and
compatible positive uncertainty scale. Unit `1` does not make it Gaussian,
Student, z, N-sigma, probability, detection, completeness, purity, peak, or
catalog authority. Detailed empirical coefficient and standardization methods
remain for a separately governed FLT/NOI derived-product contract.

## First-class FLT to FRUIT interface

The FLT product contract shall preserve a first-class FLT→FRUIT scientific
interface sufficient for a future FRUIT method to consume the filtered
amplitude product without reconstructing undocumented FLT implementation
state. At minimum, the interface shall identify the filtered quantity,
template, response, valid support, units/calibration, method/state identity,
uncertainty availability, and provenance. Additional FLT state required by an
authorized FRUIT method shall remain exactly available or reconstructable
through the product lineage.

This interface does not select a FRUIT parent, algorithm, source model,
subtraction/add-back rule, recurrence, learning, convergence, stopping,
restart, uncertainty method, or interpretation. FRUIT receives its own future
contract tranche and remains the authority for those decisions.

The implementation-blind FLT author shall preserve this interface as a first-
class scientific requirement in both contract views without inventing FRUIT
science. Exact persistence or reconstruction alternatives shall share option
identities and consequences across those views where author development is
needed.

This decision authorizes no historical global rescaling, coefficient mutation,
significance product, FRUIT method, numerical route, or Stage B launch.
