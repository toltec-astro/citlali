## Existing bindings that must not be silently reused

The admitted boundary `SCI-PTC_TO_SCI-MAP_BOUNDARY v0.2-draft.1/r0.4`
changes only the coefficient slot and inherits other frozen MAP rules. It is
not an already completed incorporation of OD-001--007/009. The successor must
identify every changed MAP source and create the necessary new boundary/source
binding records without mutating the admitted pair or retroactively rebinding
past evaluations. Preserve unchanged policy predicates; a changed predicate
needs its own exact successor rather than reuse of an older evaluation.

The same inventory applies to the AST original-footprint boundary and to
MAP-owned occurrence/coadd profiles. New binding to a changed MAP source does
not by itself alter AST coordinates, PTC production, VAL evaluation mechanics,
or JINC/NOI science. The author must report the exact required binding changes
and any real contradiction, not invent a global migration or aliases.
