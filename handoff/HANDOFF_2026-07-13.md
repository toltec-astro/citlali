  observation placeholder with an identified observation sequence and an
  optional coadd record.

  effective pixel size, required logical map-product count, and completion.

  sidecar describes only the final iteration.

  stages return successfully. CLI run completion throws on missing,

  count. They do not depend on FITS packing or Beammap split-file layout.

  completion flags, and realized totals while retaining version-1 support for
