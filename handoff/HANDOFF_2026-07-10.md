# Handoff 2026-07-10

## 09:07 EDT - NGC4449 convolve/WF OMP crash

- Context: NGC4449 `redu15` crashed after `map filtering complete for filtered coadded maps map 1/3` and immediately after `filtering noise ... (n_noise=32)`.
- The crash path is the OpenMP Wiener/convolve noise-filter path: `Engine::run_wiener_filter()` enters `#pragma omp parallel for` over noise realizations and calls `WienerFilter::filter_noise_threadsafe()`.
- Root cause identified in `include/citlali/core/mapmaking/wiener_filter_omp.h`: `filter_maps()` can invalidate the template FFT caches during convolve weight propagation, then the first parallel noise-filter calls race while rebuilding shared `filter_template_fft*` matrices and validity flags.
- Local fix applied: serialize `get_filter_template_fft()` and `get_filter_template_fft_scaled()` shared cache construction/mutation with a named OpenMP critical region `wfTemplateFFTCache`.
- Not locally built or reduction-tested; per repo guidance, validate on Unity.
- Separate config note: the `redu15` raw coadd pathology was traced to `timestream.processed_time_chunk.clean.mask_radius_arcsec: 30`, which masks samples before mean subtraction/PCA cleaning. The active NGC4449 `70_reduce.yaml` was restored to `mask_radius_arcsec: 0` outside the repo tree.
