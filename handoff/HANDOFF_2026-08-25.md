replay's required
`toltec_commissioning_science_152390_stats.nc` contained 124-by-5,518 matrices

histogram, map-diagnostic, and PSD products were populated. The process exit,
canonical bounded provenance, and recursive index checks were all valid, but
checksums can only prove artifact integrity; they cannot prove that a required
scientific product contains meaningful values. The 08:45 record below is
therefore retained as execution history, not as the current completion
candidate.

The defect was an orchestration omission rather than an observed-data
pathology. The native Science branch returned before the established
`diagnostics.calc_stats(ptcdata)` call. Pointing called it on legacy PTC state
before substituting the native prepared PTC as the map input. Native science
therefore wrote zeros, and native pointing could write diagnostics for the
wrong state.
