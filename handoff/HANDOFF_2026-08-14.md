### Owner direction and scientific purpose

Create a new durable MAP audit -> bounded repair -> independent re-audit
product for producing an observation/reduction-specific residual spatial
transfer function with final OOF maps when the Citlali source kernel is
enabled. The provisional package ID is `SCI-MAP-003`; the audit manager must
verify that the ID remains unused before registration.

LMTOOF generates a trial optical point-source image from the LMT aperture,
illumination/edge taper, Citlali passband, and trial aberrations. Citlali's
product will let LMTOOF apply the estimated residual reduction response to
each trial image before comparing it with the final Citlali OOF map. The
comparison remains in the convolved/filtered frame. Citlali does not
deconvolve the observed map.

The no-transfer path already finds useful OOF solutions, as judged by
telescope gain. The new product is therefore a forward-model refinement whose
candidate benefits are improved terminal gain, correction stability, and
number of OOF correction cycles. It is not a prerequisite for the existing
OOF method to function.

OOF may be repeated after applying a telescope correction. A new transfer is
produced from each new OOF observation/reduction and remains fixed throughout
the corresponding LMTOOF optimization. As the telescope approaches a good
solution, its PSF approaches the nominal APT-Gaussian tracer and this
approximation is expected to improve. Trial aberrations must never be used to
construct or update the transfer within an LMTOOF solve.

The audit framework says to split a package when a component has an

### Agreed estimator

For one exact product cell, let:

- `g[x,y]` be the exact discrete map-domain representation of the known
  unit-source APT Gaussian tracer before the residual Citlali analysis
  response;
- `k[x,y]` be the final normalized Citlali kernel associated with the final
  OOF map, after the realized RTC, PTC, JINC, and fruit-loop state; and
- `G[u,v]` and `K[u,v]` be their two-dimensional discrete Fourier transforms
  under one declared centering, padding/window, pixel-scale, axis, and FFT
  normalization convention.

The proposed transfer estimator is

\[
H[u,v] = \frac{G[u,v]^* K[u,v]}{|G[u,v]|^2}
       = \frac{K[u,v]}{G[u,v]}
\]

only on a preregistered valid denominator domain. LMTOOF then predicts the
map-frame model

\[
P_{\mathrm{pred}}(\theta)
  = \mathcal F^{-1}\!\left[H\,\mathcal F(P_{\mathrm{opt}}(\theta))\right].
\]

This quotient identifies a response operator from a known input/output pair;
it is not authorization to divide the observed OOF map by `H`.

### Required estimator and product rules

1. Construct `g` from the actual discrete tracer contract, not only the
   continuous analytic Gaussian transform. Bind the APT rows or fitted source
   widths, detector population, band/map identity, pixel sampling, center,
   amplitude convention, and the implemented three-sigma truncation. If the
   effective reference is a detector-weighted mixture rather than one shared
   Gaussian, define and persist that mixture explicitly.
2. Consume Citlali's final normalized kernel directly. Do not repeat a
   synthetic JINC convolution or apply a second global JINC normalization.
3. Compute and retain the complex two-dimensional transfer initially.
   A real-only or radial product requires an explicit centering/symmetry
   contract, admitted tolerances for the imaginary/anisotropic residual, and
   a separately identified derived-product status.
4. Define the denominator admission rule using `|G|^2` relative to a declared
   reference and the LMTOOF-consumed spatial-frequency domain. Frequencies
   outside the admitted band are invalid or explicitly tapered; they are not
   unstable finite estimates.
5. Preserve the measured DC/amplitude response. Do not normalize `H(0)` to
   one unless a separately approved LMTOOF amplitude contract preserves the
   removed scalar response as its own product.
6. Use explicit `ifftshift -> fft2 -> fftshift` and frequency-bin conventions.
   Bind shape, padding, cropping/windowing, WCS axes, pixel scale, units, and
   Hermitian/real-inverse expectations.
7. Bind the product to exact observation/reduction, TolTEC band and map slot,
   APT/detector selection, RTC/PTC/JINC requested/effective/resolved/realized
   state, source/validity masks, fruit-loop iteration/pass and terminal state,
   parent map/kernel identity and digest, application SHA, algorithm version,
   denominator policy, valid-frequency mask, and completion status.
8. Distinguish at least `computed_published`,
   `not_computed_or_not_requested_for_this_product`, `invalid`, and
   `unavailable`. Kernel-disabled, missing-parent, identity-mismatch, invalid
   denominator, and incomplete-product cases must fail closed according to
   an explicit required/optional publication policy.
9. The ordinary claim is a map-center, linearized residual transfer for the
   exact OOF/JINC product cell. It is not automatically an off-center,
   spatially varying, extended-source, arbitrary-morphology, nonlinear,
   cross-band, cross-mode, or universal transfer function.

The audit must decide the persisted representation and LMTOOF exchange
contract. A likely representation is a two-dimensional complex response plus
frequency coordinates and a validity mask, but this handoff does not approve
specific FITS HDU names, dtypes, radial compression, or sidecar format.

### Minimum preregistered local truth cases

The audit should define these cases before source inspection; execution waits
for the appropriate phase authorization:

1. identity: `k = g` gives unity complex transfer on the valid domain;
2. known scalar response: `k = a g` recovers the signed scalar including DC;
3. shifted response: a known subpixel/integer shift recovers the expected
   phase ramp and real inverse prediction;
4. box convolution recovers the discrete sinc response;
5. synthetic JINC convolution agrees with an independently evaluated radial
   Hankel transform in the admitted symmetric limit;
6. asymmetric known filter recovers a two-dimensional complex transfer and
   exposes loss from an unauthorized real/radial projection;
7. forward closure: applying `H` to `g` reconstructs `k` within preregistered
   error on the valid band, with excluded-frequency residual reported
   separately;
8. denominator boundaries immediately below, equal to, and above the admitted
   threshold, including zero, subnormal, NaN, and infinity;
9. odd/even shapes, FFT centering, WCS-axis orientation, pixel scale,
   padding/cropping, and Hermitian symmetry; and
10. deliberate parent/configuration/band/map-slot/iteration mismatches that
    fail before a product is represented as complete.

These analytic and synthetic fixtures are expected to be inexpensive. A
Citlali reduction, broad injection campaign, LMTOOF run, telescope correction,
Unity execution, or iteration-to-gain comparison is external/costly evidence
and is not authorized by this handoff. A later controlled comparison should
retain `H=1` as the baseline and use telescope gain after correction as the
primary acceptance endpoint, with number of OOF cycles, coefficient
stability, and map/model residuals as supporting diagnostics.
