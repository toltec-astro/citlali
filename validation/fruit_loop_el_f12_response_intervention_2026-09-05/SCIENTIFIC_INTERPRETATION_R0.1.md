# EL-F12: both bounded interventions fail the approved screen

Date: 2026-09-05. Status: completed exposed-development result.
Decision basis: approved Choice A and CAP-001; registration r0.2.

**Half and Hold are both `not_promising`. Neither advances to replication.**
All eight primary trajectories completed through absolute iteration 6 and
passed their execution and compatibility gates. Both alternatives fail
scientific protections and the fixed a1400 leakage targets. The conditional
restart criterion was not met, so no restart or diagnostic replacement ran.
Total consumption is eight trajectories and 56 iteration passes.

The historical Citlali recurrence remains the control. This result supports
neither a new policy nor a qualified method. It does not reopen the closed
UID 4460 diagnosis branch or change the rejection of faster accumulation and
simple early stopping.

## What was compared

All arms used the same frozen executable, fresh observation 123424, all three
arrays, raw JINC, alpha 1, the existing complete feedback and D19 state, and
iterations 0–6. Each arm had an uninjected branch and a branch with the bound
100 mJy/beam source from iteration 1. H0 disabled the new machinery. H audited
the selector while retaining historical hard actions. Half and Hold used the
approved alternatives through the fixed horizon, with the approved two-stage
cap rule and ordinary independent exclusions taking precedence.

H0 uninjected reproduces the retained EL-F2 historical-control signal, kernel,
empirical weight and formal weight planes bitwise: 84 plane comparisons.
Both H branches reproduce their H0 counterparts in all 84 planes each, the
existing checkpoint/D19 state, ordered per-iteration learning rows and map
diagnostics. Every injected/uninjected pair matches at iteration zero. Each
alternative matches H through iteration 1, before its first action at 2.
The final separate check confirms a1100 and a1400 remain bitwise identical to
H in all four planes throughout both alternatives and both injection branches:
56 maps and 224 plane comparisons.

The owned scalar-dimension comparison repair preserved the original H
uninjected comparison failure and rechecked the existing products without a
binary change or replay. Its narrow treatment is documented in
[the repair record](SCALAR_DIMENSION_REPAIR_R0.2.md). A later reporting wrapper
used the wrong timestamp field before it launched analysis; correcting that
field changed no registered analyzer, measurement, gate or reduction. The
original wrapper is retained with the reporting sources.

## Disposition and next owner decision

Retain this as a completed negative screen of the exact r0.1/CAP-001 method.
Do not advance either alternative, run its conditional restart, or commission
independent-pointing replication on the strength of this result. Real-data
restart restoration for these candidate trajectories remains untested because
the approved scientific condition was false; synthetic active-state coverage
still passes.

The next significant owner decision is whether to commission a separate
development generation. Such a proposal would need to address the attainable
endpoint under the historical control and the gap between response risk and
beneficial action before any new experiment. Changing a selector, cutoff,
action, protection, population, input or endpoint requires that new decision;
none is supplied here. Do not reinterpret this result by relaxing its limits.

Independent pointing remains unbound and unauthorized. Any future advancing
method must be frozen and independently replicated before policy review.
There is no population false-action rate, generic result, qualified method,
Gate-D completion, Stage B, production change or Unity activity in EL-F12.
All earlier reduction products, the original comparison failure, both
registrations and the two untracked review archives are preserved.
