# Fit-gate source-profile visualization failure 05

## Confirmed trigger

The owner-run ObsNum 150818 fit gate completed successfully on Unity on
2026-08-12 after 1,868.66 seconds. Its checksum-bound machine gate passed all
structural checks. The lag fit reported `+5.162391 ms`; that fitted value was
not used to identify or justify this repair.

Owner inspection of page 3 of
`lissajous_fit_gate_o150818.pdf` found that the purported source profiles did
not resemble compact-source crossings. The page was generated by
`scan_aggregate_profile`, which averages all eligible focal-plane detectors at
the same recorded timestamp. Detector focal-plane offsets cause their source
crossings to occur at different timestamps and angles, so this averaging
mixes on-source and off-source samples and smears the source. It repeats the
previously identified inappropriate detector-averaging failure.

## Boundary and disposition

The numerical objective does not perform this averaging. It constructs a
time-by-detector source template, profiles one nonnegative amplitude per
detector, forms detector-specific residuals, and only then sums weighted
squared residuals. Therefore this failure invalidates the compact-source
adequacy evidence on page 3, not the recorded numerical fit.

The original fit gate remains immutable. A separate renderer authenticates
the gate and reconstructs its recorded lag objective before creating any new
evidence. It aligns each eligible detector/scan crossing by signed projected
distance along that detector's local trajectory, renders direction-separated
stacks, and shows 16 individual detector crossings. It does not refit tau or
modify the numerical implementation, protocol, support, pilot, or production
Citlali code.

A synthetic regression uses two identical Gaussian crossings separated in
timestamp. Common-timestamp detector averaging reduces their peak below
`0.51`; source-aligned stacking restores a peak above `0.99` at zero projected
distance. The remaining 65 fit gates remain blocked until the supplementary
ObsNum 150818 evidence is owner-reviewed.
