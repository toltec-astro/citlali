# Guarded future cleanup plan — not currently eligible

No cleanup is authorized by this package or by MAP-UNITY-ED2. Both full PTC
captures must remain available through the fresh MAP re-audit and every
requested focused expansion.

Only after the coordinator records explicit acceptance of that re-audit may a
human prepare a separate acceptance record naming its digest and the exact
three owner-bound roots. Before any removal, the human must verify that:

1. the acceptance record says cleanup is eligible and binds the returned
   evidence digest;
2. the three paths exactly equal `capture_point_root`,
   `capture_science_root`, and `compact_evidence_root` in the frozen owner
   values;
3. every path is absolute, non-root, distinct, beneath the frozen request
   root, and not a symlink;
4. a final logical/allocated inventory and checksum manifest have been
   retained outside those targets; and
5. the coordinator separately authorizes the exact removal command.

There is deliberately no executable cleanup command here. Constructing or
executing one before those post-re-audit conditions would violate the owner
decision.
