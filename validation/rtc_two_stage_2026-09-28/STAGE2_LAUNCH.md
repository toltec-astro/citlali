# Initial Stage 2 launch gate

The driver permits exactly B-REF through the existing preparation and serial
RTC-once pipeline. It uses the original strict no-replacement counter until
an infrastructure defect actually requires a separately tested same-role retry;
this deliberately grants no automatic replacement path. Any failure stops the
driver. No Q preparation or launch is implemented before full B comparison.
The prior failed campaign retained bytes count against the shared 30 GB limit.
Both prior records and original inputs enter the protected-input ledger.
The full comparator requires every retained native hash, M0–T5 planes,
candidates, discrete admission/measurement and conditional endpoint records.
Expected source/request/publication identities may change; science tolerances
and exact discrete checks do not. One B launch is consumed even if it fails.
