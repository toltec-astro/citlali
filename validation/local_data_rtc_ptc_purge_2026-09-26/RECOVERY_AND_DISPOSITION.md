repository /Users/gwilson/GitHub/citlali/.git, even though the owned working checkout
is /private/tmp/citlali-point-additive-20260923. The exact pre-deletion source commit

RECOVERY_RECORD_INDEX.json.gz maps 87,940 original compact scientific/configuration/
manifest records to 71,969 content-addressed members in one recovery archive. Its

then extract only its `sha256/<digest>` member identified in the index. This never
restores deleted processing samples or launches numerical work.

configurations, source/runtime identities, scripts, seeds and original manifests
where present. Large arrays/maps remain in their original locations if not targeted;
the archive is not another copy of their bulk. Header structure for every NetCDF
