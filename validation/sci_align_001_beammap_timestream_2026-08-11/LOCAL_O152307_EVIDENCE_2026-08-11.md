## Numerical gate history

Two invalid executions were rejected before interpreting timing:

1. Revision 1 stopped every optimizer at iteration zero because the native
   objective was approximately `8e-15`; an independent tau grid had a lower
   objective away from the retained start.  Revision 2 introduced one fixed,
   parameter-independent signal-energy normalization.
2. Revision 2 moved spatial parameters but left tau exactly at a multistart
   seed because its scalar finite-difference step meant `1e-4 ms` for tau.
   The independent profile again disagreed.  Revision 3 adopted the pointing
   fitter's validated `0.01 ms` tau step.

Both repairs were motivated by documented numerical/profile failures, not by
the value or desirability of the fitted timing.  The synthetic and existing
pointing regression suites passed after each repair.  Only revision 3 is used
below.

## Revision-3 result

The detector-map zero-lag coordinate reconstruction gate passed exactly under
the telescope-plus-pointing, physical-APT-offset-suppressed contract.

| Model | Objective | Point estimate |
|---|---:|---|
| static | 0.1741590 | delta x = -0.049 arcsec, delta y = -2.043 arcsec |
| common lag | 0.1540964 | direct `t+tau` tau = **+18.435 ms** |
| scan-axis hysteresis | 0.1556597 | half-separation h = **-0.779 arcsec** |
| joint | 0.1534344 | tau = +29.897 ms, h = +0.532 arcsec |

The direct `t+tau` sign is opposite the retained map timing-equivalent sign.
Thus the lag-only value corresponds to a map timing equivalent of
**-18.435 ms**.  The retained negative/positive scan-rate medians were
-46.172 and +46.111 arcsec/s.  At those rates the hysteresis-only
half-separation corresponds descriptively to **-16.884 ms** in the same map
convention.

The lag profile is smooth and has its grid minimum at +17.5 ms, consistent
with the continuous lag optimum.  The joint model's lower objective but
materially shifted tau/h pair shows that nearly constant-speed, nearly pure
azimuth rasters do not strongly separate timing from scan-axis hysteresis.
No covariance, interval, or formal model-selection result is available at
this stage.

## Interpretation and next discriminating run

This real anchor shows that the direct crossing method recovers a tens-of-ms
registration effect in a Beammap, rather than the much smaller values seen in
the Lissajous pointing fits.  It therefore supports the scientific value of
the apples-to-apples test.  It does not by itself compare to a retained
left/right map estimate for the same observation, because ObsNum 152307 is not
one of the checksum-frozen split-direction corpus maps.

The smallest decisive next run is the unchanged revision-3 adapter on the
already-retained ObsNum 150819 31.4-GB full PTC and its checksum-bound standard
APT on Unity.  That observation has existing map equivalents of -26.996 ms
for the retained APT and -28.703 ms for the unthresholded full-PTC map
reconstruction.  The direct comparison is therefore explicit:

- direct tau near +27 to +29 ms would agree in magnitude and disfavor a
  factor-of-two error in the Beammap conversion;
- direct tau near +13 to +14 ms would expose a factor-of-two discrepancy;
- direct tau near zero would instead localize the effect to map formation or
  its downstream centroid estimator.

This decision table is specified before inspecting an ObsNum 150819 direct
timestream result.  No correction is authorized by any outcome.
