- normalized inner product is `0.9996376`;
- Pearson correlation is `0.9996370` and descriptive Spearman correlation is

  the largest absolute iteration-to-iteration difference is

The injected-source 20-arcsec aperture has no conditioned UID 4460 scan-5
target occurrence in either iteration. It therefore supplies no direct

## Mechanism stability

The persistence is not produced by a changing support definition. The signed-
leverage and processed-signal-contrast distributions remain close between
iterations, and the target signed-cancellation distribution is identical.
The complete-footprint signed-leverage RMS changes from `0.0146141` to
`0.0145404`; processed-signal-contrast RMS changes from `1926.11` to
`1909.81 mJy/beam`. Their spatial product therefore preserves the large
response pattern already present in iteration 4.
